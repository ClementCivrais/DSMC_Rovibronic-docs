Wall models and surface processes

Verified: the scattering and sink walls added to the solver (per-mode accommodation, radiative equilibrium, outgassing, pumping, absorption, ion neutralisation, collision-induced desorption, activated sticking), the corrected Cercignani-Lampis-Lord (CLL) wall on a moving surface, and the coverage kinetics of the coverage wall. Against: analytic references (free-molecular Fourier and Couette fluxes, radiative-equilibrium roots, decay rates, mean-field rate equations), SPARTA [SPARTA_gsi] twins (Fourier, Couette) and PICLas 4.2.0 twins.

The species ladders of the analytic references are read from the solver’s own species cache, so the references use the energies the solver uses. Unless stated otherwise the gas is free-molecular (\(\mathrm{Kn} \gtrsim 100\)) in a 1 mm cube.

Per-mode accommodation (V1)

O2 at 6000 K (the a and b states populated) strikes a 300 K wall once, and the re-emitted molecules leave the domain. The measured coefficient per mode is \(\alpha_m = (E_{in} - E_{out})/(E_{in} - E_w)\).

Cross-mode effects are physical: vibrational accommodation at fixed \(J\) raises the rotational energy slightly (measured rotational \(\alpha = -0.034\) at \(\alpha_V = 1\)), and the electronic draw at fixed \((v, J)\) gives a vibrational \(\alpha = -0.011\) at \(\alpha_E = 1\). With the inflow at the wall temperature (3000 K) and every coefficient at 0.5, the net over incident energy flux is -0.008 % (translation), +0.15 % (rotation), -0.015 % (vibration) and +0.85 % (electronic), each within 1.5 standard errors of zero.

Fourier and Couette flows (V2, V3)

Plate 1 at \(T_1\) carries the model under test; plate 2 at \(T_2\) is fully accommodating. The Fourier reference is \(q_m = \Gamma a_m [e_m(T_1) - e_m(T_2)]\) (for CLL \(q_{tr} = \Gamma(\alpha_n + \alpha_t) k \Delta T\)); the Couette reference is \(\tau = \Gamma m s U\) with plate 1 moving at 500 m/s.

Sinks, quenching, outgassing and pumping (V4, V6-V8, V11)

A face that removes or quenches a molecule with probability \(\alpha\) drains the gas of the diffuse cube at \(k = c(\alpha)\,\alpha\nu\), with \(\nu = \bar c A/(4V)\) and \(c(\alpha)\) from an independent test-particle Monte Carlo of the same cube (1.0002, 1.0015, 1.0031, 1.0110 at \(\alpha\) = 0.01, 0.02, 0.03, 0.1, each \(\pm 0.0005\)).

Reproduce

Cases and scripts are in run/GSI/boundaryConditions/<test>/ (accommodation, twoPlate, quenching, radiativeEquilibrium, outgassingPump, coverageKinetics, ionAbsorption), one run_*.py per test: run generates and runs the cases, analyse prints the comparison. The decay reference is tools/cube_decay_mc.py and tools/decay.py; the SPARTA twins are in twoPlate/sparta/ and the PICLas inputs in piclas/. The figures are drawn by run/GSI/boundaryConditions/figures/fig_gsi_bc.py and fig_gsi_chem.py. Full note: docs/Development/Verification/gsi/boundary_conditions.md.
