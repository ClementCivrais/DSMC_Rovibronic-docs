B8: multi-region conjugate solid

Verified: the conjugate solid of dsmcFoamAblation as one or more solid regions of the gas case, on the gas runTime with their own physical clock, coupled through a face-area-weighted AMI (non-conformal walls allowed), in parallel, with \(k(T)\) and \(c_p(T)\), several couplings per case, and the melt film and droplets; the coupling stays inside OpenFOAM. Against: the former single-region layout (round-off), exact solutions, serial runs (for the parallel runs), and the two verification cases of Cao and Ma [CaoMa2025_gsi].

The two layouts agree every window to 5e-13 (wall temperature) and 2e-12 (melting speed). Two processors (gas wall and solid wall on different processors, so the AMI is distributed) agree with serial to 10-15 to 5e-11, and a restart at a third of the run is bitwise identical to the uninterrupted run (400 ledger lines, final temperatures and points).

On a melting cylinder under a prescribed flux (both meshes moving, AMI remade every window) the solid region agrees with the former layout to 5e-13 (\(T_w\)) and 2e-12 (recession); in parallel with the solid solvers converged to 10-18 it agrees with serial to round-off. In the DSMC O stream the lumped heating rate is 4388.6 K/s against \(2\langle q\rangle/(\rho c_p R)\) = 4387.5 K/s (ratio 1.0003, closure 2e-12) in both layouts, and the heat load of every window is the same on the gas and the solid side of the AMI. The base-class coupling window reproduces the B1 window to the 12 printed digits, so the chemical energy is counted exactly once.

Run it yourself

Five tutorials reproduce the verified numbers of this page; each ends with a check script that prints PASS or FAIL per metric against the stored reference and redraws the comparison (Verification tutorials (gas-surface interaction)):

  • tutorials/verification/gsi/B8_melt1D: the 1-D melting slab against the exact solution (melt onset, surface temperature, melting speed, energy closure); 1 core, 4 s.

  • tutorials/verification/gsi/B8_caoMaCase1: Cao and Ma’s copper plate against the semi-infinite solution on their mesh; 1 core, 1 s.

  • tutorials/verification/gsi/B8_meltFilmShear: the shear-driven film against the exact thickness profile, film mass closure; 1 core, 31 s.

  • tutorials/verification/gsi/B8_dropletStream: the droplet in the DSMC O stream against the free-molecular ODE, with the spread of five seeds as tolerance; 1 core, 2 min.

  • tutorials/verification/gsi/B8_caoMaCase3 (long): Cao and Ma’s case 3, stagnation heat flux and heat load against their SPARTA, the temperature rise at 0.744 s against their 1.12 K; 2 cores, 2 h 12 min. The acceptance run on the main build gave 42 582 ± 296 W/m2 at the stagnation point (1.043 times their SPARTA), a heat load of 1.018 and 1.141 K; its stagnation flux is 3.9 % above the verified run of the B8 branch build.

Run ./Allrun in the tutorial directory (with the OpenFOAM environment of dsmcFoam+ sourced); the last line is PASS: n/n metrics pass and the figure is written to plots/. ./Allclean restores the tutorial.
