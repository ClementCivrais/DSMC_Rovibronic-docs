B6: production surface mechanisms

Verified (stage B6a): two production mechanisms written as native surfaceMechanisms entries, the air-carbon ablation (ACA) model of Prata, Schwartzentruber and Minton [PR22_gsi] (20 reactions: 13 GS, 7 PS) and the O-carbon model of Swaminathan-Gopalan et al. [SGC18_gsi] (6 GS, 3 PS), together with in-repository Python oracles of both models. Against: the model curves of the papers, extracted from the journal PDFs (PR22: the vector paths of Figs 1-9; SGC18: the markers of Figs 8 and 9), and the oracles’ own \(k(T)\) for the parser check. No C++ was changed in this stage; the DSMC reactor comparison (B6b) is open.

ACA (PR22)

The oracle is PR22 as written: Table 1, one site set (\(B\) = 10-5 mol m-2), the 44 277 K O(s) desorption energy, like-pair LH events at the rate \(k[X]^2\) with two adsorbates removed per event, and \(T_{gas} = T_s\).

Two molecular-beam conventions appear in the paper (Fig. 1 at fixed pressure and \(T_{gas} = T_s\); Figs 3, 4, 8a and 9 with the concentration at 1000 K), and each figure reproduces only with its own. Not reproduced, and left open without tuning: the O(s) desorption knee of the “[G] at 1000 K” figures (up to 0.0125 in probability near 1400 K), and Fig. 7 (O2 reactivity), whose paper/oracle ratio is \(\sqrt 2\) at 4 Pa and grows to 1.84 at 6.79e-6 Pa and 2400 K. The transients end on the closed-form steady state to 10-15.

SGC18

Interpretations (stated in the mechanism file): O’ is the total O-containing coverage; \(P_{ads}\) = 0.85 is constant, the branch being drawn with weight \(w_i/\sum w\) given adsorption (SG25 two-level scheme [SG25_gsi]); no site limit in the mean field.

  • Imposed coverage. At each temperature \(\theta'\) is inferred from the Fig. 9 model O fraction and the six-channel split is predicted: it reproduces Fig. 8 to the reading precision of the data, 0.02-0.04 (largest deviation 0.038, slow CO{b} at 900 K).

  • Continuous beam. No constant flux reproduces the pulsed-beam numbers of Fig. 9 over 740-1876 K; 2e18 m-2 s-1 follows Fig. 9 within 0.04 from 740 to 1400 K. A Langmuir reading of the adsorption entry was tested and rejected (smallest \(x_O\) 0.562 at 740 K against 0.244 in the paper).

Mechanism files and parser check

mechanisms/acaPR22 (normalisation sparta, stoichiometry power, GS reactions in the efficiency form, the spectator rows 3, 7, 12, 17 and 20 marked spectator true) and mechanisms/oCarbonSGC18 (normalisation sg25, \(\Phi\) = 3.5e19 m-2, TD products with the td kernel). One dsmcFoam step on a one-cell box whose six faces are mechanism-mode coverage walls (ACA at 800, 1500, 2400 K; SGC18 at 700, 1000, 1875 K): 87/87 reaction-temperature rows PASS, largest relative difference of \(k(T)\) 4.7e-10 (the ten significant digits of the dictionary coefficients). The check passes again with the computed reaction heats of B5a.

DSMC verification (B6b)

The B6b engine runs the two mechanisms in DSMC. It adds the PR22 spectator channels: a GS reaction whose gas reactant reappears unchanged among its products (rows 3, 7, 12, 17, 20, e.g. O + O(s) + C(b) \(\rightarrow\) CO + O) is an independent event induced by the impact, not a fate of the impinging parcel.

  • Each impact of species G adds \(P_r W\) to a per-element carry of every spectator channel \(r\) of G (\(W\), the parcel weight).

  • Whole events of the element weight fire from the carry.

  • The impinging parcel’s own fate is drawn from the other channels.

  • When both happen in one impact, a fair coin sets their order.

Both hosts emit the events: the coverage wall and the immersed surface. A host that cannot emit them stops with an error.

The adsorbate binding energies of the ACA are not the PR22 desorption activation energies, which make several steps endothermic beyond their activation energy:

  • O(s) and O*(s): 33 301 K, from the microscopic reversibility of the forward and reverse pairs (rows 9/8 and 16/19);

  • N(s): 11 070 K, the upper bound set by row 12.

The steady wall heat does not depend on them (to \(5\times10^{-14}\)).

Run it yourself

The tutorial tutorials/verification/gsi/B6a_mechanismParser runs the parser check of this page: one dsmcFoam step with the two mechanism files, whose printed reactions, terms and \(k(T_w)\) are compared with the oracle values stored in its reference/ (87 rows, relative tolerance 5e-9). ./Allrun takes a few seconds on one core and ends with PASS: 89/89 metrics pass. Stage B6b (engine runs of the mechanisms against the papers’ figures) is not covered yet.

The ACA in DSMC under the molecular beams of Minton’s group (the continuous beams it was fitted to and the earlier pulsed beams) is Beam consistency of DSMC with the ACA mechanism; its application to a receding carbon cylinder in dissociated air is Application: carbon cylinder under the ACA mechanism.
