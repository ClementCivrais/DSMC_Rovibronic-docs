Gas-surface interaction verification

The present section collects the verification of the gas-surface interaction (GSI) framework of Wall and inflow models: wall scattering and accommodation, probability-based and finite-rate surface chemistry, the time integration of the pure-surface reactions, the scattering kernels of reaction products, the heat of reaction, the receding surfaces (body-fitted, implicit and explicit immersed) and the conjugate solid of dsmcFoamAblation. Each page states what is verified and against what (an analytic oracle, SPARTA [SPARTA_gsi], PICLas [Lauterbach2025_gsi] or a published case), summarises the set-up, reproduces the key numbers of the verification note in the source tree and gives the paths needed to rerun the case.

The work was organised in blocks (B0-B8) after an audit of the state of the art (docs/Development/GSI_SOTA_AUDIT_20260929.md); the design decisions of each block are recorded in docs/Development/gsi_specs/ and the full verification notes in docs/Development/Verification/gsi/. Every case, oracle and compact result lives under run/GSI/; the run directories were deleted after harvest, so the pages quote the numbers written in the notes.

Conventions

  • ours designates dsmcFoam+; ratios are ours/reference unless stated otherwise.

  • Errors quoted with \(\pm\) are standard errors (of a mean over seeds, of batch means or Poisson errors, as stated on each page); \(z\) is the deviation in standard errors.

  • Every figure follows the same style: dsmcFoam+ in red markers, the reference as black lines (or SPARTA as black symbols).

  • A seed is an independent replica set with randomSeed N; in constant/dsmcProperties.

Cost

Speed was a requirement of every block. The measured overheads are summarised below; details are on the block pages.

Item

Measured cost

Per-face wall temperature, uniform mode (B1)

+0.20 % instructions per step on a wall-dominated case (38 instructions per impact)

Finite-rate GS selection (B2)

20-30 ns per impact, under 1 % of the step cost per impact

PS integration, per active element and step (B3)

exponential 0.55 µs (4 reactions, full Jacobian), timeCounter 0.05 µs, stochastic 0.455 µs; inactive elements cost nothing

Internal-state draw at a diffuse wall (B4)

0.6 µs (O2) and 0.2 µs (N2) per impact, against 190 µs and 383 µs with the former rejection sampler

Immersed chemistry (B5b)

3413-3440 s per immersed run against 3449-3499 s for the same case on a body-fitted wall

State-resolved layer (B11b)

below the run-to-run noise (\(< 0.3\ \mu\)s per impact); start-up tables 0.4 s for the ACA
