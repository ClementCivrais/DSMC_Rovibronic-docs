Wall models and surface processes
=================================

**Verified:** the scattering and sink walls added to the solver
(per-mode accommodation, radiative equilibrium, outgassing,
pumping, absorption, ion neutralisation, collision-induced
desorption, activated sticking), the corrected Cercignani-Lampis-Lord
(CLL) wall on a moving surface, and the coverage kinetics of the
coverage wall. **Against:** analytic references (free-molecular
Fourier and Couette fluxes, radiative-equilibrium roots, decay
rates, mean-field rate equations), SPARTA [SPARTA_gsi]_ twins
(Fourier, Couette) and PICLas 4.2.0 twins.

The species ladders of the analytic references are read from the
solver's own species cache, so the references use the energies the
solver uses. Unless stated otherwise the gas is free-molecular
(:math:`\mathrm{Kn} \gtrsim 100`) in a 1 mm cube.

Per-mode accommodation (V1)
---------------------------

O\ :sub:`2` at 6000 K (the a and b states populated) strikes a 300 K
wall once, and the re-emitted molecules leave the domain. The
measured coefficient per mode is
:math:`\alpha_m = (E_{in} - E_{out})/(E_{in} - E_w)`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 60 40

   * - Coefficient swept from 0 to 1 (others 0)
     - Worst :math:`|\alpha_{meas} - \alpha_{in}|`
   * - :math:`\sigma` (momentum)
     - 0.0013
   * - :math:`\alpha_T`
     - 0.0012
   * - :math:`\alpha_R`
     - 0.0016
   * - :math:`\alpha_V`
     - 0.0019
   * - :math:`\alpha_E`
     - 0.0030

Cross-mode effects are physical: vibrational accommodation at fixed
:math:`J` raises the rotational energy slightly (measured rotational
:math:`\alpha = -0.034` at :math:`\alpha_V = 1`), and the electronic
draw at fixed :math:`(v, J)` gives a vibrational
:math:`\alpha = -0.011` at :math:`\alpha_E = 1`. With the inflow at
the wall temperature (3000 K) and every coefficient at 0.5, the net
over incident energy flux is -0.008 % (translation), +0.15 %
(rotation), -0.015 % (vibration) and +0.85 % (electronic), each
within 1.5 standard errors of zero.

----------------------------------

Plate 1 at :math:`T_1` carries the model under test; plate 2 at
:math:`T_2` is fully accommodating. The Fourier reference is
:math:`q_m = \Gamma a_m [e_m(T_1) - e_m(T_2)]` (for CLL

The former CLL wall applied the kernel to the laboratory velocity
and added :math:`\alpha_t U_w`, so a moving wall accommodated
:math:`\alpha_t = \sigma_t(2 - \sigma_t)` instead of
:math:`\sigma_t`; the kernel now acts on the velocity relative to
the wall. SPARTA's ``surf_collide cll ... translate`` adds the full
wall velocity after a laboratory-frame kernel, so its partially
accommodating moving CLL wall accommodates fully. PICLas 4.2.0 shows
the same frame error on its Maxwell wall (MomentumACC 0.5 gives
:math:`s` = 0.9999, ratio 2.000).

Sinks, quenching, outgassing and pumping (V4, V6-V8, V11)
---------------------------------------------------------

A face that removes or quenches a molecule with probability
:math:`\alpha` drains the gas of the diffuse cube at
:math:`k = c(\alpha)\,\alpha\nu`, with :math:`\nu = \bar c A/(4V)`
and :math:`c(\alpha)` from an independent test-particle Monte Carlo
of the same cube (1.0002, 1.0015, 1.0031, 1.0110 at
:math:`\alpha` = 0.01, 0.02, 0.03, 0.1, each :math:`\pm 0.0005`).

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 55 45

   * - Test
     - Result
   * - Electronic quenching, :math:`\alpha_E` = 0.01 / 0.03 / 0.1
     - :math:`k/k_{ref}` = 1.0051 / 0.9971 / 0.9934
   * - Vibrational deactivation, :math:`\alpha_V` = 0.01 / 0.03 / 0.1
     - 0.9912 / 1.0013 / 0.9994
   * - Outgassing into a closed box (N\ :sub:`2`)
     - :math:`N(t) = \dot m A t/m` to +0.04 %
   * - Blowing into an open channel (Ar, :math:`U_b` = 0, 300,
       1000 m/s)
     - exact transit inventory to about 1 %
   * - Constant-:math:`\alpha` pump (Ar)
     - 1.0025 (:math:`\alpha` = 0.02), 1.0013 (0.1)
   * - Pumping-speed mode
     - decay at :math:`S/V`: 1.0074 (statistical error 0.45 %)
   * - Vacuum chamber, outgassing against a PI pump (target 0.02 Pa)

       captured/emitted = 1.0013

       :math:`\phi` = 4.5 eV)

       decay 0.9958 (0.02), 0.9988 (0.1)

PICLas 4.2.0 twins (one hexahedral cell) give
:math:`T_w` = 2100.66 / 1185.99 K against 2100.72 / 1186.02 K
(ratio 0.99997), Fourier 1.0004 / 1.0014 / 1.0004, quenching
0.995-0.999, pump 1.0004 and outgassing 1.0000.

Coverage kinetics (V10)
-----------------------

The coverage :math:`\theta(t)` printed by the coverage wall is
compared with the mean-field rate equations including the
depletion of the gas inventory (these checks were repeated through
the finite-rate engine in :doc:`b3_ps_integration`).

.. figure:: ../../_static/figures/gsi/gsi_chem_verification.png
   :width: 100%
   :align: center

   Surface chemistry against its references: (a) Park oxidation
   efficiency, (b) catalytic :math:`\gamma(T_w)` for RCC and
   SiO\ :sub:`2`, (c) Langmuir adsorption and desorption,
   (d) activated sticking, (e) collision-induced desorption,
   (f) second-order Langmuir-Hinshelwood, (g) Eley-Rideal,
   (h) ion neutralisation energy and wall share.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 55 45

   * - Process
     - Agreement
   * - Langmuir adsorption + Polanyi-Wigner desorption
       (:math:`\theta_{eq} \approx 0.5`)
     - rms :math:`\Delta\theta` = 0.009
   * - Activated sticking :math:`S_0 e^{-600/T_w}` at 300 / 600 /
       1200 K
     - fitted :math:`S_0`: 0.971, 0.993, 1.036 of the analytic value
   * - Collision-induced desorption Ar(g) + O(s) → Ar + O(g)
     - rms :math:`\Delta\theta` = 0.005
   * - Second-order Langmuir-Hinshelwood,
       :math:`\theta_0/(1 + 2k\theta_0 t)`
     - exact (deterministic step)
   * - Eley-Rideal CO(g) + O(s) → CO\ :sub:`2`\ (g)
     - rms :math:`\Delta\theta` = 0.007

Reproduce
---------

Cases and scripts are in ``run/GSI/boundaryConditions/<test>/``
(``accommodation``, ``twoPlate``, ``quenching``,
``radiativeEquilibrium``, ``outgassingPump``, ``coverageKinetics``,
``ionAbsorption``), one ``run_*.py`` per test: ``run`` generates and
runs the cases, ``analyse`` prints the comparison. The decay
reference is ``tools/cube_decay_mc.py`` and ``tools/decay.py``; the
SPARTA twins are in ``twoPlate/sparta/`` and the PICLas inputs in
``piclas/``. The figures are drawn by
``run/GSI/boundaryConditions/figures/fig_gsi_bc.py`` and
``fig_gsi_chem.py``. Full note:
``docs/Development/Verification/gsi/boundary_conditions.md``.
