Three-dimensional immersed sphere and SPARTA twin
=================================================

**Verified:** the immersed finite-rate engine in three dimensions, at fixed
shape and under recession, on the implicit surface (marching tetrahedra)
and the explicit surface (triangulated STL). **Against:** the other
representation, and SPARTA's ``surf_react adsorb`` [SPARTA_gsi]_ in a
one-to-one twin on the same triangles. Results and scripts:
``run/GSI/Article_JCP/RESULTS_3D_beam.md`` section 1 and
``run/GSI/Article_JCP/sphere3d/``.

Set-up
------

* Carbon sphere, R = 0.5 mm at (1.5 mm, 0, 0), in the O stream of
  :doc:`immersed_surface` (4 km/s, 1000 K, :math:`2\times10^{22}`
  m\ :sup:`-3`); quarter domain 4 :math:`\times` 2 :math:`\times` 2 mm with
  symmetry planes y = 0 and z = 0, 80 :math:`\times` 40 :math:`\times` 40
  cells (R/h = 10); wall at 2000 K.
* Coverage mechanism ``b5bcyl`` of :doc:`b5b_immersed_chemistry`
  (adsorption, Eley-Rideal, direct oxidation; desorption, oxidation of
  O(s), Langmuir-Hinshelwood), :math:`10^{19}` sites/m\ :sup:`2`.
* Explicit surface: the 20 480-triangle icosphere; :math:`5\times10^{8}`
  atoms per parcel, 3000 steps of 5 ns, two seeds per surface.

Fixed shape: implicit against explicit
--------------------------------------

Window 3-15 µs, whole-sphere values; :math:`\pm` standard error of batch
means.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 50 50

   * - Quantity
     - explicit / implicit
   * - Carbon removal
     - 0.9985 :math:`\pm` 0.0027
   * - O impacts
     - 1.0020 :math:`\pm` 0.0016
   * - Mean O(s) coverage
     - 0.9987 :math:`\pm` 0.0020
   * - The six reactions
     - 0.9942-1.0010

The coverage falls from 0.43 at the stagnation point to 0.15 at 75-90°
and below 0.005 beyond 120°; the bulk ledger equals the oxidation tallies
to 1.000000 in every run.

SPARTA twin (1280 triangles)
----------------------------

SPARTA's coverage is per triangle: on the 20 480-triangle sphere one parcel
would fill a third of a triangle's sites. The twin therefore uses the
level-3 icosphere (1280 triangles of about one cut cell), the ``b5bcyl``
reactions in SPARTA's files, read by dsmcFoam+ through its SPARTA import
(combinatorial stoichiometry, ``timeCounter``), SPARTA 27 Aug 2026,
two seeds each.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 50 50

   * - Quantity (3-15 µs)
     - dsmcFoam+ / SPARTA
   * - Adsorption
     - 1.0069 :math:`\pm` 0.0019
   * - Eley-Rideal
     - 1.0068 :math:`\pm` 0.0081
   * - Direct oxidation
     - 1.0025 :math:`\pm` 0.0044
   * - Desorption
     - 1.0053 :math:`\pm` 0.0035
   * - Oxidation of O(s)
     - 1.0021 :math:`\pm` 0.0052
   * - Langmuir-Hinshelwood
     - 0.9953 :math:`\pm` 0.0089
   * - Mean coverage
     - 1.0014 :math:`\pm` 0.0028
   * - O impacts
     - 1.0081 :math:`\pm` 0.0017

Every channel and the coverage agree within 0.7 %. The O impacts of
dsmcFoam+ are 0.8 % above SPARTA's (4.8 standard errors), and adsorption
and Eley-Rideal follow them. **The cause has not been found**; the two
codes differ in their gas collision models (rovibronic Larsen-Borgnakke
against SPARTA's rotational relaxation) and in the collision volumes of
the cut cells.

*Discreteness of the inventory.* With SPARTA's combinatorial LH
convention, pairs are counted among 5-20 simulated O(s) per element, and
the recombination proceeds at 0.62 of the mean-field rate of the native
mechanism (power form): coverage +2.3 %, carbon removal +3.8 %. The same
family of effect appears on the ACA cylinder of :doc:`aca_cylinder`, where
fewer than one simulated O*(s) per element and a single-quantum removal
probability above one double the O*(s) coverage.

Recession
---------

Solid density 1800/1.5e5 kg/m\ :sup:`3`, 15 µs, two seeds per surface; the
sphere loses 31 % of its volume.

* Swept / removed volume: 1.000000 (implicit, both seeds, at 7.5 and
  15 µs), 0.999998-1.000008 (explicit).
* Stagnation depth at 15 µs: 192.0 µm (implicit), 191.9 µm (explicit);
  largest difference 7.4 µm at 30° (0.15 cell), seed spread up to 4 µm.
* No parcel inside the solid, no cell that lost gas, no hand-over of the
  explicit surface.

No SPARTA reference exists for this recession: SPARTA runs its site
chemistry on explicit surfaces only, and ``fix ablate`` recedes implicit
surfaces only.

   carbon (ledger) against the swept solid of both surfaces.

Run it yourself
---------------

Two tutorials in ``tutorials/verification/gsi/`` reproduce this page on
one core (:doc:`../../guide/verification_tutorials`):
``sphere3dTwin`` (the SPARTA twin, about 43 min) and ``sphere3dImplicit``
(the implicit surface at fixed shape against the stored explicit runs,
about 44 min). The beam reactor of :doc:`beam_consistency` is
``acaBeamReactor``. The full set:
``run/GSI/Article_JCP/sphere3d/run_sphere3d.sh``, ``run_twin.sh`` and
``sparta/run_sparta.sh``, then ``analyse_sphere3d.py``.
