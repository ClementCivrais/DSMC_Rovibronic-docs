B5a: reaction heats and chemistry-driven recession
==================================================

**Verified:** every gas-surface (GS) and pure-surface (PS) event of
the finite-rate engine releases its 0 K heat of reaction

.. math::

   \Delta E = \sum h_f(\text{reactants}) - \sum h_f(\text{products}),

computed from the formation enthalpies of the frozen species
database; the coverage wall recedes from the bulk ledger of its
surface state, on the moving body-fitted mesh of
``dsmcFoamAblation`` and on the conjugate solid; the ablating wall
splits the recombination energy with the gas as the catalytic wall
does. **Against:** hand values from the raw database sources, exact
energy and atom ledgers, and the exact solution of the 0-D rate
equations.

Reaction heats
--------------

Gas neutrals take the database formation enthalpy; an ion its
neutral plus the ionisation potential; a bulk element 0 (graphite:
:math:`h_f(\mathrm{C, gas})` = 711.749985 kJ/mol); an adsorbate
:math:`h_f(\text{gas}) - kE_a` of its desorption, else its
``bindingEnergy``. An independent oracle computes every
:math:`h_f` from the raw sources (ATcT rows and the ground-term
offsets of the frozen database):

Energy ledger closure
---------------------

Closed column of O and O\ :sub:`2` (5e21 m\ :sup:`-3` each,
3000 K) above a coverage wall at 2000 K with a mixed mechanism (AA O,
DA O\ :sub:`2`, ER, oxidation by O and O\ :sub:`2`; PS desorption,
second-order LH, oxidation of O(s), sublimation), 2000 steps:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 50 25 25

   * - Identity
     - exponential
     - stochastic
   * - chemical-energy ledger against :math:`\sum \Delta E_r\,
       N_r`
     - 2.7e-14
     - 4.9e-14
   * - total energy (gas kinetic, internal and :math:`h_f`, adsorbed
       and carried :math:`h_f`, wall heat :math:`q_{BF} + q_{chem}`),
       relative to the wall heat
     - 2.3e-14
     - 9.5e-15
   * - kinetic plus internal energy against the solver's "Total
       energy"
     - 1.8e-15
     - 1.8e-15

The ablating wall (O → O\ :sub:`2`, :math:`\gamma` = 0.3,
:math:`\beta_{acc}` = 0.3 and 1) closes its own identity, gas plus
wall energy changing by :math:`h_f(\mathrm{O})(N_{events} -
N_{partners})`, to 5.5e-14 and 7.0e-14 of the wall heat.

.. figure:: ../../_static/figures/gsi/b5_energy_closure.png
   :width: 65%
   :align: center

   Change of each energy term over the run of the mixed GS/PS
   closed case (kinetic, internal, :math:`h_f` of the gas, adsorbed
   and carried, wall heat :math:`q_{BF} + q_{chem}`) for the
   exponential and stochastic PS integrators; the total is zero to
   round-off.

0-D recession on the moving body-fitted wall
--------------------------------------------

O beam (10\ :sup:`20` m\ :sup:`-3`, 4000 m/s, 1000 K) onto a graphite
plate at 2000 K with a linear mechanism (AA :math:`S_0` = 0.5, direct
oxidation 0.05, desorption, oxidation of O(s), sublimation),
``dsmcFoamAblation`` with ``ablationRecession { patch wall;
accelerationFactor 1e6; }``. Oracle:
:math:`\Phi\,d\theta/dt = F S_0(1 - \theta) - (k_d + k_o)\Phi\theta`,
:math:`v_{rec} = m_C j_{C,ss}/\rho_s`, with the measured O impact
flux :math:`F`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 55 45

   * - Quantity
     - DSMC/exact
   * - Steady recession velocity (second half)
     - 0.99988
   * - Coverage (after the end-of-step sampling factor 0.998507)
     - 0.99971 :math:`\pm` 0.00015
   * - Removed mass at 500 µs
     - 0.99984
   * - Wall points moved against :math:`10^6` times the ledger depth
       (3.46e-4 m)
     - 0.999998890206
   * - Direct-oxidation probability
     - 0.050016 (input 0.05)

The measured flux is 7.2 % above the analytic beam flux :math:`nU`
because the gas re-emitted by the wall builds up in the column; the
oracle therefore takes the measured flux.

.. figure:: ../../_static/figures/gsi/b5_recession.png
   :width: 55%
   :align: center

   Coverage, recession depth and the ratio DSMC/exact of the moving
   body-fitted wall against the exact solution of the rate
   equations; the early deficit is the flux transient while the
   re-emitted gas builds up.

Conjugate run with a finite-rate wall
-------------------------------------

The B8 multi-region column with a coverage wall (same mechanism, the
element temperature from the solid face) and a graphite-like slab
(:math:`\rho` 1800, :math:`c_p` 710, :math:`k` 100, :math:`T_0`
2000 K), 20 windows: the solid recession equals
:math:`\sum_w v_{chem,w}\,t_{s,w}` to 1.3e-15 and the gas-side bulk
ledger :math:`\times\, m_C/\rho_s` (scaled by the solid/gas window
time) to 5.6e-15; the solid wall points move by the recession to
1e-12; the region energy closure is :math:`\le` 2.0e-12 and the
chemical ledger equals :math:`\sum \Delta E\,N` to 2.1e-13.

.. figure:: ../../_static/figures/gsi/b5_conjugate.png
   :width: 60%
   :align: center

   Finite-rate wall coupled to the conjugate solid: recession of the
   solid region (ledger) against the gas-side bulk ledger
   :math:`\times\,m_C/\rho_s` (top); wall temperature and wall heat
   :math:`q_{BF} + q_{chem}` over the 20 windows (bottom).
