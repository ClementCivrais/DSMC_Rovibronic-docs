B0: correctness of the surface state
====================================

**Verified:** three correctness fixes without new physics. The
coverage wall conserves the adsorbed mass exactly (no clamp of the
coverage), the reactive walls write and read their state on restart,
and the probability of the immersed surface follows the wall
temperature. **Against:** round-off identities (atom balance, restart
continuation) and the analytic :math:`\beta(T_w)`.

D1: exact site gating
---------------------

A parcel adsorbs only if its face holds the free sites it needs (two
per molecule for dissociative adsorption), and an Eley-Rideal event
occurs only if the adsorbed atoms are present; otherwise the parcel
is reflected and counted as rejected.

Closed 1 mm box of O with a sticking wall (:math:`S_0 = 1`, no
desorption), the site density chosen so that one parcel fills 0.3 of
the face (:math:`W = 10^6`, :math:`n_{sites}` = 3.333e6):

* the face saturates at :math:`\theta` = 0.9 (three parcels), and 55
  later adsorption draws are rejected;
* O balance: 104 parcels (1.04e8 atoms) at :math:`t = 0`; at
  2e-5 s, 101 parcels in the gas plus 3e6 adsorbed atoms, a relative
  difference of 0.0;
* the former clamp would have taken the face to 1.0 and destroyed
  2e5 atoms.

D2: restart of the wall state
-----------------------------

The coverage wall writes its state, and the ablating wall its
per-face carbon ledger, at every write time (processor-local files
under ``<time>/uniform/dsmcWalls/``; since B2 the coverage wall
writes ``<patch>SurfaceState``). The saturation case restarted from
1e-5 s resumes at :math:`\theta` = 0.900000000009 instead of 0, and
its tallies continue.

D3: immersed probability follows :math:`T_w`
--------------------------------------------

The immersed slab with radiative equilibrium, :math:`A = 0.5` and
:math:`T_a = 3000` K:

   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 35 35

   * - :math:`T_w` [K]
     - :math:`\beta`
     - :math:`\beta/(0.5\,e^{-3000/T_w})`
   * - 656.825
     - 5.192336e-03
     - 0.999999999996
   * - 551.841
     - 2.177668e-03
     - 0.999999999997
   * - 516.439
     - 1.500199e-03
     - 1.000000000004
   * - 509.290
     - 1.382719e-03
     - 1.000000000002

Before the fix :math:`\beta` stayed at :math:`0.5\,e^{-3}` = 0.0249.

Regression: the second-order Langmuir-Hinshelwood case
``coverage_lh2`` (:math:`k` = 2e4 s\ :sup:`-1`,
:math:`\theta_0 = 0.8`) gives :math:`\theta(10^{-4}\,\mathrm{s})` =
0.190455 against the exact :math:`0.8/(1 + 2k\theta_0 t)` = 0.190476,
the explicit-Euler error of the step at the time (since B3 the
integration is exact: 0.1904761917).
