B11b: state-resolved gas-surface layer
======================================

**Verified:** the state-resolved layer on finite-rate surface mechanisms
(``stateResolved true;`` in a native mechanism entry, class
``stateResolvedLayer``). The rates stay the mechanism's; each impinging
particle meets the threshold of every activated impact step with its own
energy, and the gas products leave in definite rovibronic states.
**Against:** a Python oracle of the layer as coded, on the level ladders
written by each run (``run/GSI/B11b_state_layer/oracle/b11b_oracle.py``),
the ACA steady state of :doc:`b6_mechanisms` [PR22_gsi]_, and the same
runs with the layer off. Full note:
``docs/Development/Verification/gsi/b11b_state_layer.md``.

Model
-----

* **Impact steps.** A step :math:`r` with :math:`E_{a,r} > 0` that is not
  a spectator channel has the per-impact probability

  .. math::

     P_r(i) = C_r(T_s)\,F_r\,\Theta(E_n + \varepsilon_i - E_{a,r}),\qquad
     C_r = \min\!\left(1, \frac{\gamma_r(T_s)}{\langle\Theta\rangle_r(T_s)}\right),

  with :math:`E_n = m (\mathbf{U}\cdot\mathbf{n})^2/2`, :math:`\varepsilon`
  the rovibronic energy of the parcel's level, :math:`\gamma_r` the
  mechanism's rate factor, :math:`F_r` its coverage factor, and
  :math:`\langle\Theta\rangle_r = \sum_l g_l e^{-\max(\varepsilon_l,
  E_{a,r})/kT}/q(T)` the fraction of the equilibrium impinging flux above
  the threshold. At :math:`T_{gas} = T_s` the flux average is the
  mechanism's probability. :math:`\langle\Theta\rangle_r(T)` is tabulated
  at start-up and :math:`C_r` is cached per element with the rate
  constants; a :math:`C_r > 1` is capped and reported. Spectator channels
  and steps with :math:`E_a = 0` are unchanged.
* **Thermal products** (pure-surface reactions): the equilibrium flux at
  :math:`T_s` inside the window :math:`[E_{rev}, \infty)` of the reverse
  impact step (level weight :math:`g e^{-\max(\varepsilon, E_{rev})/kT_s}`,
  :math:`E_n = \max(0, E_{rev} - \varepsilon) + \mathrm{Exp}(kT_s)`); without
  a reverse step, the detailed-balance mirror :math:`E_{rev} = E_{a,f} -
  \Delta H` (flagged).
* **Direct products** (gas products of the impact steps): microcanonical
  sharing of :math:`E_{avail} = E_n + \varepsilon_{in} + \Delta E` over
  the products' levels and translations, then the lattice share
  :math:`f = 4\mu/(1+\mu)^2` of each product's normal translation to the
  wall; the per-event ledger closes to round-off.
* **Re-emission.** An unreacted parcel leaves from
  :math:`\Phi(1-S)/\int\Phi(1-S)`, :math:`S` the state-dependent sum of the
  channel probabilities.
* Exact samplers, :math:`O(\log N)` per draw; no run-time loop over faces
  or levels. Both hosts: the coverage wall (fixed or moving) and the
  immersed surface.

Flags and open items
--------------------

* For atoms :math:`C_r = A` up to the excited electronic levels of the
  ladder (:math:`\le 3\times10^{-4}` at 3000 K).
* Spectator channels are exactly those of B6b (state independent, their
  product kernels kept); the induced-event product energy of the B11a note
  (section 10.6) is not implemented.
* CO\ :sub:`2` products (PR22-4, PR22-18) keep their kernels (polyatomic
  products are not resolved); the N\ :sub:`2` and CN windows of PR22-14 and
  PR22-15 are detailed-balance mirrors (no reverse step in the ACA).
* Two-product direct channels, ``normalisation sg25`` with the layer and
  SGC18 with the layer are not verified here.
