B2: finite-rate gas-surface engine
==================================

**Verified:** the surface-mechanism language (site sets, adsorbates,
bulk species, catalysts, both stoichiometry conventions, the SPARTA
and SG25 normalisations, Kisliuk forms and rate forms), the SPARTA
``surf_react adsorb`` file import, and the gas-surface (GS) selection
of ``surfaceState`` on the coverage wall. **Against:** SPARTA
(27 Aug 2026, ``spa_serial``) on its own adsorb examples, with the
mechanism files imported verbatim, and mean-field rate equations
(0-D oracle).

The parse test reads SPARTA's ``sample-GS_1/2`` and ``sample-PS_1/2``
verbatim: 16/16 reactions match id, type, terms, :math:`\alpha`,
kernels and :math:`k(1000\,\mathrm{K})` (relative difference
:math:`\le` 10\ :sup:`-10`). The atom balance of the smoke test is 0
(SPARTA import, with restart) and 1.9e-15 (native mechanism), and the
heat-of-reaction ledger equals :math:`W \sum n_r \Delta E_r` to
10\ :sup:`-15`.

SPARTA beam twin
----------------

SPARTA ``examples/surf_react_adsorb/in.beam.surf.gs``: two O beams on
a plate with the CLL kernel ``cll 300 0.5 0.5 0.5 0.5`` and
``fnum 1e6``. Ours: the plate as two faces, the beams as cold O
inflows giving the same 31.416 impacts per step and element; impacts
per element and run, SPARTA 28604 (sd 81) against ours 28586 (sd 4).
Variant (b) uses a moderated mechanism (``sample-GS_2m``) with
:math:`n_{sites}` = 1.2e8 m\ :sup:`-2`.

counts within 3 standard errors (end values within 0.7 %). Variant
(e) is the deliberate deviation: with :math:`k_{CI}` = 10\ :sup:`8`

O(s). SPARTA executes them all and clamps the negative count at the
next sync, creating 3930 O atoms per element and run; ours rejects
the draws the inventory cannot supply and creates none.

0-D rate-equation oracle
------------------------

Closed box (L = 10 mm) with the reacting face at :math:`x_{min}`,
the other faces specular, a diffuse default kernel at 1000 K, 1000
simulated sites; the ODE writes the per-impact probabilities
independently with the impingement rate
:math:`N\sqrt{kT/(2\pi m)}/L`.

engine.

Cost
----

1 mm cube, 1e5 O parcels, every face the tested wall, 172 122 impacts
per step: the mechanism mode costs 20-30 ns per impact over the
diffuse wall (3280 ns per impact for the whole step), the same as
the former coverage wall. The selection is O(n) in the channels of
the impinging species, and the rates are cached per element and
recomputed only when its temperature changes.
