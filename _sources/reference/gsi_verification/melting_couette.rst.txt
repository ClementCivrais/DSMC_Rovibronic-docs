Application: melting slab under a rarefied Couette flow
=======================================================

**Shown:** the conjugate solid of ``dsmcFoamAblation`` coupled to the DSMC
gas through the coupling windows, on the supersonic Couette flow of argon
over a melting slab of Bariselli [Bariselli2020_melt]_ (thesis Sec. 6.6.1,
Appendix E). **Against:** the slip-regime solution of Appendix E (oracle
written for any viscosity law, reproduces Table 6.5 and the solid fraction
4.44 % with the thesis gas) and the value reported in the thesis. Results,
scripts and the full numbers: ``run/GSI/Article_Melting/RESULTS.md``
(section 1).

Set-up
------

* Gap 0.1 m, upper wall at 2000 K sliding at 832.95 m/s, diffuse; slab of
  1.02 mm with its base at 300 K; Kn 0.01 on the gap,
  :math:`n_0` = 1.742e21 m\ :sup:`-3`.
* Condensed phase (Table 6.3): :math:`\rho_c` 1e3 kg/m³, :math:`\nu_l`
  1e-8 m²/s, :math:`\lambda_l` 1e-4 and :math:`\lambda_s` 1e-5 W/(m K),
  :math:`T_m` 900 K.
* **Deviation 1, gas model:** the frozen species database gives the
  Ar-Ar VSS pair (:math:`\omega` 0.71967, :math:`\alpha` 1.46794), not
  the thesis VHS with :math:`\omega` = 1; same :math:`n_0`. The slip
  solution is solved for both gases: the gas model moves the shear by 9 %
  but the heat flux and the solid fraction by < 1 %.
* **Deviation 2, liquid model:** the liquid conducts (95 % of the slab,
  2190 K at its surface), which the isothermal melt film cannot hold. The
  steady state is computed with the solid region alone, :math:`k(T)`
  switching from :math:`\lambda_s` to :math:`\lambda_l` at :math:`T_m`,
  Stefan path off (no latent heat enters a steady state). The liquid
  surface velocity :math:`V_i = \tau D_l/\eta_l` is imposed through the
  frame (upper wall at :math:`V_u - V_i`), updated between run segments.
* Three replicas (seeds 1-3), 2 ms of gas spin-up, then eight segments
  of 10 ms (50 000 DSMC steps, 25 000 windows of 0.04 s of solid time);
  the first two segments are left out. One serial core each, about 5.5 h.

Window stability
----------------

The window coupling is explicit, and the wall flux responds at once to
:math:`T_w` through the re-emitted molecules, :math:`h = 2k_B\Gamma`
(12.8 W/(m² K) here). A window of solid time :math:`\Delta t_w` is
stable when

.. math::

   2h\left(\frac{\Delta t_w}{\pi k\rho c_p}\right)^{1/2} < 1 .

With :math:`\lambda_l` = 1e-4 W/(m K) the left-hand side is 4.6 for 10 s
windows (the first attempt diverged in five windows) and 0.29 for the
0.04 s windows used. The number of windows per conduction time
:math:`D_c^2/(\pi^2\alpha)` does not depend on :math:`\rho c_p`: about
26 000 here.

The shear (hence :math:`V_i`) is 3 % above the first-order slip solution,
the gas 10-15 K hotter at its peak and the velocity jumps 13 % larger:
expected differences between the Boltzmann gas and a Navier-Stokes
solution with first-order jumps and first-order Chapman-Enskog transport;
not adjusted. Cost: 3 :math:`\times` 5.5 h serial, against about 20 days
for the DSMC-SPH run of the thesis.

Metal bars (Leiser et al. 2022)
-------------------------------

The Al 6060 and AA7075 bars of Leiser et al. [Leiser2022_melt]_ have been
run as 1-D slabs under the measured PWK4 heat fluxes, with the melt
removed. The final numbers will be published after the melt-through fix
of the solid region; ``tutorials/verification/gsi/MELT_leiserBar``
checks the energy balance of the 65 km case.
