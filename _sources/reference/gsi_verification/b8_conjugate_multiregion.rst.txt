B8: multi-region conjugate solid
================================

**Verified:** the conjugate solid of ``dsmcFoamAblation`` as one or
more solid regions of the gas case, on the gas ``runTime`` with their
own physical clock, coupled through a face-area-weighted AMI
(non-conformal walls allowed), in parallel, with :math:`k(T)` and
:math:`c_p(T)`, several couplings per case, and the melt film and
droplets; the coupling stays inside OpenFOAM. **Against:** the former
single-region layout (round-off), exact solutions, serial runs (for
the parallel runs), and the two verification cases of Cao and Ma
[CaoMa2025_gsi]_.

The two layouts agree every window to 5e-13 (wall temperature) and
2e-12 (melting speed). Two processors (gas wall and solid wall on
different processors, so the AMI is distributed) agree with serial to
10\ :sup:`-15` to 5e-11, and a restart at a third of the run is
**bitwise identical** to the uninterrupted run (400 ledger lines,
final temperatures and points).

On a melting cylinder under a prescribed flux (both meshes moving,
AMI remade every window) the solid region agrees with the former
layout to 5e-13 (:math:`T_w`) and 2e-12 (recession); in parallel
with the solid solvers converged to 10\ :sup:`-18` it agrees with
serial to round-off. In the DSMC O stream the lumped heating rate is
4388.6 K/s against :math:`2\langle q\rangle/(\rho c_p R)` = 4387.5 K/s
(ratio 1.0003, closure 2e-12) in both layouts, and the heat load of
every window is the same on the gas and the solid side of the AMI.
The base-class coupling window reproduces the B1 window to the 12
printed digits, so the chemical energy is counted exactly once.

The restart of the shear film is bitwise identical. On a curved wall
in parallel the film agrees with serial to round-off until dripping
starts, then within 2e-3: a serial run with the heat flux perturbed
by 10\ :sup:`-14` departs by the same amount, so this is the
threshold scatter of the dripping process, not a parallel defect.

Non-conformal walls (cylinder under a prescribed flux, gas wall of 80
faces): :math:`\langle T_w\rangle` at 31 ms is 815.908 K (80 solid
faces), 815.690 K (120), 815.615 K (160) and 815.544 K (320), second
order in the solid resolution; the non-conformal pair 80/160 is
identical to the conformal 160/160. The gas wall points stay within
10\ :sup:`-18` m of the solid wall where they coincide with solid
points, and the heat load is mapped conservatively (gas side against
solid side within 6e-16).

Cao and Ma case 3: Mach-10 argon over a copper cylinder
-------------------------------------------------------

Lofthouse's Kn 0.01 case as in Cao and Ma section 3.3: Ar,
:math:`n` = 4.237e20 m\ :sup:`-3`, 200 K, 2634 m/s, cylinder
D = 0.3048 m, diffuse wall, copper at 500 K, no radiation; 24 windows
of 400 DSMC steps each driving 31 solid steps of 1 ms (solid time
0.744 s); one replica on 2 processors.

     - 1.8e-11

The heat flux lies between the references, which differ by 9 %
among themselves. The temperature rise is 2.4 % below Cao and Ma's,
inside the 1.037-1.136 K spread that the reference stagnation fluxes
give. Deviations from their set-up: VSS from the frozen database
instead of their VHS parameters, and a coarser wall mesh (1.25 mm).
