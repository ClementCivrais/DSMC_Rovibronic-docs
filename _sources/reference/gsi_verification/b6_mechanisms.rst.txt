B6: production surface mechanisms
=================================

**Verified (stage B6a):** two production mechanisms written as native
``surfaceMechanisms`` entries, the air-carbon ablation (ACA) model of
Prata, Schwartzentruber and Minton [PR22_gsi]_ (20 reactions: 13 GS,
7 PS) and the O-carbon model of Swaminathan-Gopalan et al.
[SGC18_gsi]_ (6 GS, 3 PS), together with in-repository Python oracles
of both models. **Against:** the model curves of the papers,
extracted from the journal PDFs (PR22: the vector paths of Figs 1-9;
SGC18: the markers of Figs 8 and 9), and the oracles' own
:math:`k(T)` for the parser check. No C++ was changed in this stage;
the DSMC reactor comparison (B6b) is open.

ACA (PR22)
----------

The oracle is PR22 as written: Table 1, one site set
(:math:`B` = 10\ :sup:`-5` mol m\ :sup:`-2`), the 44 277 K O(s)
desorption energy, like-pair LH events at the rate
:math:`k[X]^2` with two adsorbates removed per event, and
:math:`T_{gas} = T_s`.

Two molecular-beam conventions appear in the paper (Fig. 1 at fixed
pressure and :math:`T_{gas} = T_s`; Figs 3, 4, 8a and 9 with the
concentration at 1000 K), and each figure reproduces only with its
own. Not reproduced, and left open without tuning: the O(s)
desorption knee of the "[G] at 1000 K" figures (up to 0.0125 in
probability near 1400 K), and Fig. 7 (O\ :sub:`2` reactivity), whose
paper/oracle ratio is :math:`\sqrt 2` at 4 Pa and grows to 1.84 at
6.79e-6 Pa and 2400 K. The transients end on the closed-form steady
state to 10\ :sup:`-15`.

SGC18
-----

Interpretations (stated in the mechanism file): O' is the total
O-containing coverage; :math:`P_{ads}` = 0.85 is constant, the branch
being drawn with weight :math:`w_i/\sum w` given adsorption (SG25
two-level scheme [SG25_gsi]_); no site limit in the mean field.

* **Imposed coverage.** At each temperature :math:`\theta'` is
  inferred from the Fig. 9 model O fraction and the six-channel split
  is predicted: it reproduces Fig. 8 to the reading precision of the
  data, 0.02-0.04 (largest deviation 0.038, slow CO{b} at 900 K).
* **Continuous beam.** No constant flux reproduces the pulsed-beam
  numbers of Fig. 9 over 740-1876 K; 2e18 m\ :sup:`-2` s\ :sup:`-1`
  follows Fig. 9 within 0.04 from 740 to 1400 K. A Langmuir reading
  of the adsorption entry was tested and rejected (smallest
  :math:`x_O` 0.562 at 740 K against 0.244 in the paper).

Mechanism files and parser check
--------------------------------

``mechanisms/acaPR22`` (``normalisation sparta``, ``stoichiometry
power``, GS reactions in the efficiency form, the spectator rows 3,
7, 12, 17 and 20 marked ``spectator true``) and
``mechanisms/oCarbonSGC18`` (``normalisation sg25``,
:math:`\Phi` = 3.5e19 m\ :sup:`-2`, TD products with the ``td``
kernel). One dsmcFoam step on a one-cell box whose six faces are
mechanism-mode coverage walls (ACA at 800, 1500, 2400 K; SGC18 at
700, 1000, 1875 K): **87/87 reaction-temperature rows PASS, largest
relative difference of** :math:`k(T)` **4.7e-10** (the ten
significant digits of the dictionary coefficients). The check passes
again with the computed reaction heats of B5a.

Open for the DSMC stage (B6b): engine support for the PR22 spectator
channels (their per-impact sums of 1.25-1.93 are renormalised by the
``sparta`` normalisation, so the ACA file is not yet for
production), and the SGC18 impulsive-scattering and TD-barrier
parameters, which are not in the paper (placeholders in the parser
check).
