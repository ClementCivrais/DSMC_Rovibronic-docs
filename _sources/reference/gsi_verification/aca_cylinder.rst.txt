Application: carbon cylinder under the ACA mechanism
=====================================================

**Shown:** the immersed finite-rate engine on a production mechanism in a
flow field: a carbon cylinder ablating under the 20 reactions of the
air-carbon ablation (ACA) mechanism [PR22_gsi]_ in a dissociated-air
stream, on the implicit and the explicit immersed surfaces
(:doc:`immersed_surface`, :doc:`b5b_immersed_chemistry`). **Against:** the
other surface representation on the same mesh, and the closed-form
steady state of the mechanism at the incident fluxes measured in each
sector of the surface (oracle of :doc:`b6_mechanisms`). Results, scripts
and the full numbers: ``run/GSI/Article_JCP/application/RESULTS.md``.

Set-up
------

* Cylinder of radius 0.5 mm, centre (1.5 mm, 0), in a 4 :math:`\times` 4 mm
  box of 80 :math:`\times` 80 cells of 50 µm (0.1 mm slab), the geometry
  of :doc:`b5b_immersed_chemistry`; implicit surface (marching squares)
  or explicit surface (SPARTA's 180-segment circle as an STL).
* Stream: 4 km/s, 1000 K, :math:`2\times10^{22}` m\ :sup:`-3`, mole
  fractions O 38/127, O\ :sub:`2` 2/127, N 16/127, N\ :sub:`2` 71/127
  (Kn based on the diameter 0.105, Mach 5.7). This composition is a
  representative frozen dissociated air chosen for the application
  (19/21 of the O\ :sub:`2` and 8/79 of the N\ :sub:`2` dissociated); it
  is **not** taken from a flight or a facility condition.
* Wall at a fixed 2000 K (a radiative-equilibrium wall would leave the
  validity range of the mechanism without a conducting solid).
* Mechanism ``acaPR22`` verbatim (spectator channels, O(s), O*(s), N(s),
  nitridation to CN, the adopted binding energies), ``elementTallies
  true``.
* Recession accelerated by :math:`F = 1.5\times10^5` (solid density
  1800/F kg/m\ :sup:`3`): the physical recession rate is the simulated
  one divided by :math:`F`, i.e. 0.094 mm/s at the stagnation point.
* :math:`2.5\times10^{8}` atoms per parcel (155 000 parcels), 3000 steps
  of 5 ns (15 µs), LB-VHS collisions, two seeds per surface.

Closed-form check per sector
----------------------------

``closed_form_check.py`` recovers the incident O, N and O\ :sub:`2`
fluxes of each 15° sector from the events of the channels that consume
the impinging species (rows 1, 4, 5; 10, 13; 16, 18, 19), whose
per-impact probabilities are known functions of the coverages, and
solves the steady state of the mechanism at 2000 K with these fluxes.
The recovered fluxes reproduce the measured reactive impacts within 2 %
on the windward side (8 % on the lee side) and the whole-surface split
O/N/O\ :sub:`2` of the impact counters within 0.004.

* Carbon removal, DSMC / closed form: 0.981 over the body, 0.95-1.00 per
  sector.
* O(s) and N(s) coverages: within the seed spread.
* O*(s) coverage: 1.5-2.3 times the closed form at every angle
  (resolution limit, below).

   :width: 85%

   the closed form, and DSMC over the closed form against the probability

Resolution limit of the inventory (O*(s))
-----------------------------------------

An element holds :math:`N_q = \Phi A_e/W \approx 104\text{-}139` adsorbate
quanta at full coverage; the closed-form O*(s) coverage is 0.4-0.7 quanta
per element. The Eley-Rideal removal of O*(s) (row 7) has the per-impact
efficiency :math:`1000\,e^{-4000/T} = 135` at 2000 K, so the probability
that one O impact removes a lone quantum is :math:`135/N_q = 1.1\text{-}1.6`,
above one, whereas an impact cannot remove more than the element holds.
In the four runs, row 7 fired 40 845-41 024 spectator events and rejected
42 493-44 331 on an empty inventory (row 20: 3 016-3 137 fired,
6 492-6 935 rejected). The O*(s) inventory rises until its removal
balances its adsorption.

The carbon removed through O*(s) is not affected: at steady state it
equals the O*(s) adsorption rate, :math:`0.7\,\theta_E` per O impact,
which does not depend on :math:`\theta_{O^*}`. The departure grows with
the single-quantum probability (1.48 at 1.11, 2.2-2.3 at 1.5-1.6), while
O(s), held at 0.06-1.2 quanta per element but removed with a probability
of 0.12-0.17, follows the closed form: the criterion is the removal
probability of a single quantum, not the number of quanta. It can be
checked from the element weight before a run. No solver change has been
made; an adsorbate weight smaller than the parcel weight of the gas would
lift the limit (outlook).

Cost
----

Same cylinder (implicit surface, 400 steps), wall = ACA, one reaction
probability (O + C(b) :math:`\rightarrow` CO), or diffuse without reaction:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 40 30 30

   * - Wall
     - time per step / diffuse, serial
     - 4 ranks
   * - one probability
     - 1.130
     - 1.166
   * - ACA (20 reactions)
     - 1.179
     - 1.183

The surface functions (impacts and PS integration of the 76 elements) take
0.1 % of the step. The difference comes from the gas phase: the reactive
walls turn atoms into molecules near the wall, and every molecular
collision copies the level tables of the species (finding recorded in
``docs/Development/Verification/perf/p4_level_table_copy.md``, change
pending).

Run it yourself
---------------

``tutorials/verification/gsi/APP_acaCylinderImplicit`` and
``APP_acaCylinderExplicit`` run this case to 4.5 µs on one core (11-13 min)
and check
removed carbon, reaction totals, coverages, recession depth and the
swept-volume identity against the article runs at 4.5 µs
(:doc:`../../guide/verification_tutorials`). The full 15 µs run:
``run/GSI/Article_JCP/application/run_aca.sh``.
