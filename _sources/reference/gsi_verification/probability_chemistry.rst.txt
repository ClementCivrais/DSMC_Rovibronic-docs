Probability-based catalysis and carbon ablation
===============================================

**Verified:** the per-impact surface reactions of the catalytic and
ablating walls (``srRecombination``, ``srFiniteRate``): the realised
reaction probability against the input :math:`\gamma` or
:math:`\beta(T_w) = \min(1, A T_w^n e^{-T_a/T_w})`, the
material :math:`\gamma(T_w)` correlations, Kisliuk precursor
sticking, the recombination-energy accommodation and the recession
velocity of the kinetic-theory flux. **Against:** the analytic
definitions, SPARTA ``surf_react prob`` and the PICLas
``CAT_RATES_ER`` regression reservoir [Lauterbach2025_gsi]_.

Catalytic recombination: SPARTA, PICLas, dsmcFoam+
--------------------------------------------------

A catalytic wall recombines impinging atoms (O + O → O\ :sub:`2`)
with probability :math:`\gamma`. SPARTA (``surf_react prob``, written
as O → O\ :sub:`2` carrier plus O → NULL partner) and dsmcFoam+
(``dsmcCatalyticWallPatch``, ``nReactantConsumed 2``) both apply the
probability per impact:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 35 35

   * - :math:`\gamma` input
     - SPARTA
     - dsmcFoam+
   * - 0.25
     - 0.254
     - 0.249
   * - 0.50
     - 0.505
     - 0.495
   * - 0.75
     - 0.749
     - 0.750

PICLas ``CAT_RATES_ER`` (Eley-Rideal, one gas atom plus one adsorbed
atom, ``nReactantConsumed 1`` in dsmcFoam+) has an effective
per-impact probability :math:`\gamma_{eff} \approx 0.0025`; its
O\ :sub:`2` build-up in a closed 10\ :sup:`-5` m cube follows the
analytic recombination relaxation
:math:`x_{O_2} = \gamma_{eff}\,\nu_w t` to within 2 %.

   :align: center

   Panels: (a) Realised against input recombination probability for SPARTA
   and dsmcFoam+ (and the PICLas effective value); (b) the PICLas

Recession from the kinetic-theory flux
--------------------------------------

For a gas at number density :math:`n` and temperature :math:`T`
striking a surface with ablation probability :math:`\gamma`, the
recession velocity is
:math:`v_{rec} = \gamma\,\tfrac14 n \bar c\, m_C/\rho_s`. Single-cell
box, O consuming C(s), :math:`n = 10^{23}` m\ :sup:`-3`, 5000 K,
:math:`\rho_s` = 1800 kg/m\ :sup:`3`:

Park oxidation and Titan nitridation efficiencies
-------------------------------------------------

With a single reactant species the measured per-impact probability
is events/impacts. O + C(s) → CO with the Park form
:math:`\beta = 0.63\,e^{-1160/T_w}` [Park1976gsi]_
(``run/GSI/parkOxidation``) and N + C(s) → CN with the low-rate

The oxidation efficiency follows the Park curve within 0-5 % (the
small positive bias is the re-impact of re-emitted O in the 0-D box).
The nitridation deviations of -13 % and +11 % at 1500 and 3500 K are
the Poisson noise of 22 and 203 events; with more than 50 events the
error is below a few per cent. The default nitridation stays the
Park-Bogdanoff constant :math:`\beta = 0.3` [ParkBogdanoff2006_gsi]_;
the Titan form is opt-in (``nitrideN_titan``).

Mars and Earth ablator cases (graphite at 2000 K, dissociated
freestream at 4 km/s) fire every channel: ``marsAblation`` gives
:math:`\dot m_C` = 0.10 kg m\ :sup:`-2` s\ :sup:`-1` (recession
5.5e-5 m/s, CO dominant), ``earthAblation`` 0.072 kg m\ :sup:`-2`
s\ :sup:`-1` (4.0e-5 m/s, CO and CN).

The correlations are those of Stewart [Stewart1997]_ (RCC) and
Scott (SiO\ :sub:`2`); catalytic :math:`\gamma` values scatter by up
to about three times between facilities, so the temperature trend
is the robust part.

Kisliuk precursor sticking,
:math:`S(\theta) = S_0/(1 + K\theta/(1 - \theta))`
(``run/GSI/catalysisKisliuk``): :math:`K = 1` reproduces the
Langmuir steady coverage to all printed digits (0.7061953582);
:math:`K = 0.2` raises it to 0.8879 and :math:`K = 5` lowers it to
0.4577. The model-independent ratio :math:`S(\theta_{ss})/\theta_{ss}`
is 0.416 / 0.416 / 0.436 / 0.419 (within about 5 %). These are the
values of the original coverage wall; the same cases run through the
finite-rate engine since B3 are given in :doc:`b3_ps_integration`.

Recombination-energy accommodation (``run/GSI/catalysisEnergy``,
O + O → O\ :sub:`2`, :math:`E_{rec}` = 8.203e-19 J): the ledger
total equals wall share plus gas share exactly for
:math:`\beta_{acc}` = 1, 0.5 and 0 (residual 0, shares 1.00 / 0.50 /
0.00 to the wall). With :math:`\beta_{acc} = 0` the O\ :sub:`2`
leaves at about 3644 m/s against about 943 m/s for
:math:`\beta_{acc} = 1`.

Reproduce
---------

dsmcFoam+ catalysis: ``run/0D/Validation/testCatalytic`` (sweep
:math:`\gamma` in ``system/boundariesDict``); SPARTA:
``sparta-master/run_catalytic/in.catalytic``. Carbon ablation:
``run/GSI/parkOxidation``, ``run/GSI/titanAblation``
(``nitridationSweep``, ``tempSweep``), ``run/GSI/marsAblation``,
``run/GSI/earthAblation``. Catalysis enhancements:
``run/GSI/catalysisGammaT``, ``run/GSI/catalysisKisliuk``,
``run/GSI/catalysisEnergy``. Notes:
``docs/Development/Verification/gsi/README.md``,
``carbon_ablation_rates.md`` and ``catalysis_enhancements.md``.
