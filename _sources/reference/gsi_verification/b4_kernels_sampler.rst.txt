B4: scattering kernels and the exact internal-state sampler
===========================================================

**Verified:** the exact O(1) sampler of the diatomic rovibronic
state at a temperature (Vose alias tables at temperature nodes with
an exact acceptance correction), its conditional and two-temperature
draws, and the SPARTA scattering kernels ``td``, ``impulsive``,
``cll`` (with ``partial``) and ``adiabatic`` for walls and reaction
products. **Against:** the exact Boltzmann populations over the
allowed levels, SPARTA's ``examples/surf_collide`` beams one-to-one,
and the analytic marginals of the product kernels.

Sampler
-------

Temperature nodes :math:`T_m = 10\,\mathrm{K}\times 1.15^m`
(:math:`m` = 0..79, up to 6.2e5 K); a query at :math:`T` uses the
smallest node :math:`T_m \ge T`. One draw takes a column and an alias
test from one uniform number and accepts the level with probability
:math:`\exp[-(E_l - E_{min})(1/kT - 1/kT_m)] \le 1`, so the accepted
level is exactly Boltzmann at :math:`T` (mean acceptance at least
:math:`1.15^{-d/2}`). Tables are built lazily per (species, level
range, node).

After the review the node constants are precomputed and a draw costs
75-121 ns. On a free-molecular cube with diffuse walls (12 000-13 000
impacts per step) the internal-state draw costs about 0.6 µs
(O\ :sub:`2`) and 0.2 µs (N\ :sub:`2`) per impact against 190 µs and
383 µs before: a diffuse wall now costs about as much as a specular
one (whole-run speed-up 75 and 168).

0.5000 :math:`\pm` 0.0010 over five seeds.

tangential, N and O) are within 0.7 % of 1, with mean :math:`z^2` 1.06. After
the review fixes, the impulsive and CLL variants give 24 ratios
within 0.8 % of 1, and every impulsive run of both codes agrees with
the kernel's exact second moments (ours: 60 values, mean :math:`z^2`
1.09).

Differences from SPARTA
-----------------------

* Discrete levels: SPARTA draws continuous rotational and
  vibrational energies, dsmcFoam+ discrete levels (``td`` draws them
  from the two-temperature Boltzmann distribution; ``impulsive
  intenergy`` takes the highest level not above the new energy by
  bisection, O(log N)).
* The soft-sphere ``impulsive`` kernel guards the two cases where
  SPARTA returns NaN velocities (a clamp of the mean final energy,
  counted and printed).
* The ``cll`` internal accommodation is a per-mode partial
  accommodation at :math:`T` (Gibbs moves of the Boltzmann
  distribution, so detailed balance holds); a
  ``maintainOddEvenParity`` species keeps the parity of :math:`J`.
