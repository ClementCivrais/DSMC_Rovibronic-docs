Melting of ice: ablating slab and Neumann slab
==============================================

**Verified:** the solid region of ``dsmcFoamAblation`` with water ice,
first with the melt removed as it forms (wall Stefan path), then with the
melt retained and conducting (latent heat as an apparent heat capacity in
the enthalpy form, :math:`c_p(T)` and :math:`k(T)` tables). **Against:**
the exact onset and pre-melting surface temperature of a semi-infinite
slab, a front-fixed finite-difference oracle, and the two-phase Neumann
solution [CJ1959_melt]_. Results, scripts and the full numbers:
``run/GSI/Article_Melting/RESULTS.md`` (section 3).

Ablating slab (melt removed)
----------------------------

Ice slab of 0.3 m (600 cells), initially at 253.15 K, under
:math:`q = 10^4` W/m² (``prescribedHeatFlux``, :math:`\varepsilon = 0`,
empty gas column), solid step 0.1 s, ten steps per window, to 4000 s.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 50 50

   * - Check
     - Result
   * - Melt onset
     - window (12, 13] s, exact 12.922 s
   * - Surface temperature before melting
     - within 0.013 K of the semi-infinite solution
   * - Melting speed at 4000 s / oracle
     - 0.999999 (steady speed 2.9061e-5 m/s)
   * - Recession at 4000 s / oracle
     - 0.999979 (0.1123 m)
   * - Energy closure
     - 1.1e-11

Neumann slab (melt retained)
----------------------------

Ice at 263.15 K melts from a face held at 283.15 K (slab 0.2 m, 2000
cells, step 1 s, 3600 s); both phases have the density of ice, as in the
exact solution; the latent heat is a trapezoid of area :math:`L` over
:math:`T_m \pm \Delta T`; the wall Stefan path is switched off.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 35 35

   * - :math:`\Delta T`
     - front / exact at 3600 s
     - max :math:`|T - T_\mathrm{exact}|`
   * - 2 K
     - 1.0932
     - 0.71 K
   * - 1 K
     - 1.0495
     - 0.42 K
   * - 0.5 K
     - 1.0259
     - 0.27 K
   * - 0.25 K
     - 1.0140
     - 0.27 K
   * - linear fit at 0
     - 1.0033

The error is that of the finite latent-heat interval: halving the cell
size or the step changes the front by less than 1e-4. The energy ledger
of this case closes only to 2-3 %, as the energy through a face held at a
fixed temperature is not recorded by the ledger of the coupled walls; the
front and the profile are unaffected.

Rerun
-----

* ``tutorials/verification/gsi/MELT_iceAblation`` (1 core, 38 s) and
  ``tutorials/verification/gsi/MELT_iceNeumann`` (1 core, 21 s):
  :doc:`../../guide/verification_tutorials`.
* Generators and oracles: ``run/GSI/Article_Melting/slab/make_slab.py``,
  ``slab/slab_oracle.py``, ``ice/run_neumann.sh``,
  ``ice/oracle/neumann.py``; compact results ``ice/results/`` (written by
  ``ice/export_ice_results.py``).

Not run: the ice spheres of Hauk and Bonaccurso (2016) and Rasmussen
et al. (1984) melt under convective and evaporative exchange in air at
1 bar, which needs a convective and evaporative wall condition and the
axisymmetric film terms first.
