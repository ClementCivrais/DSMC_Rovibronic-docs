Beam consistency of DSMC with the ACA mechanism
===============================================

**Checked:** DSMC with the air-carbon ablation (ACA) mechanism [PR22_gsi]_
under the molecular beams of Minton's group, against the closed-form
steady state of the mechanism (solver error) and against the measured
product yields (model error). The ACA was fitted to the continuous
0.36 eV O and N beams of Murray et al. (2020), so the comparison with
these data is a **consistency check, not an independent validation**. The
pulsed 5 eV O and 4.8 eV N beams were not fitted (PR22 report similar
trends) and test the mechanism outside its calibration. Results and scripts: ``run/GSI/Article_JCP/RESULTS_3D_beam.md``
section 2 and ``run/GSI/Article_JCP/beam/``.

Reactor
-------

One carbon element of 1 mm\ :sup:`2` on a coverage wall (mechanism
``acaPR22``) under a collisionless drifting beam, outgoing gas counted per
species at the top of the column, started from the closed-form steady
coverages; 25 000 steps, about 40 parcels per step, two seeds, 100 runs.

Solver: DSMC against the closed form
------------------------------------

* Every yield above :math:`10^{-3}`: DSMC / closed form 0.996-1.006, except
  the CO\ :sub:`2` efficiency of the 0.36 eV O beam at 1600 K (1.04 at
  :math:`1.4\times10^{-3}`, about 250 events).
* Gas ledger (deleted / expected) 0.9925-1.0005; coverages / closed form
  0.992-1.006.
* At the conditions of the 5 eV beam, DSMC reproduces the model curves of
  PR22 Fig. 1 to three digits (CO 0.7861 against 0.7858, O\ :sub:`2`
  0.1022 against 0.1018 at 1200 K).

The solver error is therefore separated from the model error.

Model: the mechanism against the data
-------------------------------------

* Follows the data: the O and CO efficiencies of the 0.36 eV O beam within
  40 % at every surface temperature (800-1870 K), and the N efficiency of
  the 0.36 eV N beam within a factor of two.
* Misses: the O\ :sub:`2` and N\ :sub:`2` recombination (about three times
  low; no net O\ :sub:`2` below about 1270 K), CO\ :sub:`2` above 1400 K
  (falls by :math:`10^3` from 1200 to 1800 K, the data by about
  :math:`10^2`), CN of the N beam below 1100 K (30 times low), and the
  5 eV O beam (CO about two times high, O about three times low). The
  5 eV misfit belongs to the published model: with the PR22 molecular-beam
  convention it returns 10-36 % of the O atoms as O\ :sub:`2`, which the
  normalisation O + CO + CO\ :sub:`2` does not count.

The runs with the state-resolved layer of :doc:`b11b_state_layer` are in
``RESULTS_3D_beam.md`` (not shown here). The layer acts on the normal
energy of the impact, so these runs use the incidence angles of the
experiments: 45° for Murray et al. (2015) and Murray and Minton (2019),
60° for Murray et al. (2020), with the same speed and the same flux per
unit area as at normal incidence. The ACA alone does not depend on the
angle: one point per experiment at its angle gives the yields of the
normal-incidence runs within 0.7 %.

Run it yourself
---------------

The tutorial ``tutorials/verification/gsi/acaBeamReactor`` runs the beam
reactor in about 75 s; its 21 checks lie within 4 standard errors of the
closed form (:doc:`../../guide/verification_tutorials`); the 3-D tutorials
``sphere3dTwin`` and ``sphere3dImplicit`` are on :doc:`sphere3d`. The full campaign:
``run/GSI/Article_JCP/beam/campaign_beam.py`` and ``summarise_beam.py``.
