``dsmcSpeciesDatabase`` — spectroscopic & VSS data
==================================================

Source: ``src/lagrangian/DSMC/speciesDatabase/``.

Role
----

``dsmcSpeciesDatabase`` is the single source of truth for
per-species data used throughout the solver. The data are compiled
into ``libDSMC_MNF`` and frozen: they are generated from stored
source files and cannot be overridden from a case (see
:doc:`species_database_sources`). The cloud instantiates the
database when the ``rovibronicDatabase`` sub-dictionary is present
in ``constant/dsmcProperties``; without it the solver stops. Every
downstream consumer —  collision models, reaction classes,
diffuse-wall re-sampling, and the macroscopic-temperature
inversion —  reads through the cloud's per-species constant
properties and its per-pair collision tables, both built from this
object at start-up.

Contents of a species entry
---------------------------

For each species listed in ``typeIdList`` the database supplies:

#. **Mass and VSS collision parameters** —  the mass, the reference
   diameter :math:`d_{\mathrm{ref}}`, the viscosity exponent
   :math:`\omega` and the scattering exponent :math:`\alpha`, all
   referenced to :math:`T_{\mathrm{ref}} = 273~\mathrm{K}`. The
   cloud turns them into the pair tables ``dPQ_``, ``omegaPQ_``
   and ``alphaPQ_`` (Gosma rows for neutral pairs, mean rule for a
   charged partner).
#. **Spectroscopic constants** —  one block per electronic state
   :math:`n`, containing the term energy :math:`T_e^{(n)}`, the
   harmonic frequency :math:`\omega_e^{(n)}`, the anharmonicities,
   the equilibrium rotational constant :math:`B_e^{(n)}`, the
   rotation-vibration coupling :math:`\alpha_e^{(n)}`, the
   centrifugal distortion :math:`D_e^{(n)}`, the dissociation
   energy from the potential minimum and the electronic degeneracy
   :math:`g_{\mathrm{elec}}^{(n)}`; :math:`J`-weighted levels for
   atoms; normal modes and rotational constants for polyatomics.
#. **Rovibronic level table** —  the three-dimensional
   ``internalEnergyList[n][v][J]`` in Joules, built at start-up,
   with the ground level normalised to zero.
#. **Degeneracy table** —  ``degeneracyList[n][v][J]``, which
   carries the rotational multiplicity :math:`(2J+1)`, the
   electronic degeneracy and the :math:`1/\sigma` symmetry factor.
#. **Ionisation potential** (for all species), and the
   **dissociation energy** :math:`D_0` (for molecules). These
   enter the chemistry module as the *only* heats of reaction
   that the user does not need to supply.

Level-table construction
------------------------

With ``computeEnergyLevels true`` (the default) the database
builds the level table at every start-up from the spectroscopic
constants through the expansion documented in
:doc:`../theory/rovibronic_levels`. The ladders are exact functions
of the frozen data, so two runs with the same species list always
build identical tables.

``constant/speciesCache``
`````````````````````````

When ``databasePath`` is set (conventionally
``"constant/speciesCache"``), the database writes one
``<species>.dat`` file per diatomic or atomic species (levels,
degeneracies, dissociation and ionisation energies, spin
multiplicities). The file is a **cache**, not an input: the solver
reads it back only when ``computeEnergyLevels`` is ``false``, and
it never validates it against the compiled database. After a
database change every cache that may be read, by the solver or by
a Python oracle, must be regenerated with ``dsmcInitialise``; an
old cache is renamed aside (``speciesCache.stale_<date>``), not
deleted. Polyatomic ladders are always recomputed.

Diagnostic output
-----------------

At start-up the cloud prints the collision-pair table,
``Collision pairs (d_ref [m], omega, alpha; Tref = 273 K)``, and
the level count of every species (for example
``Total (n,v,J) levels: 17389`` for O\ :sub:`2`). For each molecular
species that participates in a dissociation channel, the reaction
classes print the asymptote-matching table described in
:doc:`../theory/chemistry`. With the generated spectroscopy these
matching errors are at round-off (of order
:math:`10^{-34}~\mathrm{J}`); a larger value is symptomatic of an
inconsistent species entry and should be investigated before
proceeding.

Polyatomic species
------------------

In addition to the diatomic and atomic species, the database
ships a set of **polyatomic species** whose spectroscopic constants
are generated, like all others, into
``dsmcSpeciesDatabaseSpectroscopy.C`` from the stored CCCBDB and
ATcT sources, value by value with their provenance (see
:doc:`species_database_sources`). A species is selected simply by
adding its symbol to ``typeIdList``; the
:math:`(J, K)` rotational ladder and the multi-mode vibrational
ladders are then built at start-up by the same
``computeEnergyLevels`` path. The supported molecules and their
rotor types are:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 24 22 54

   * - ``rotationType``
     - Species
     - Rotational treatment
   * - ``linear``
     - CO\ :sub:`2`, N\ :sub:`2`\ O, HCN, C\ :sub:`3`
     - Rigid rotor, :math:`g = 2J+1`.
   * - ``sphericalTop``
     - CH\ :sub:`4`
     - :math:`g = (2J+1)^2`, :math:`\xi_{\mathrm{rot}} \to 3`.
   * - ``symmetricOblate``
     - CH\ :sub:`3`, NH\ :sub:`3`
     - :math:`(J, K)` ladder, :math:`A = B > C`.
   * - ``symmetricProlate``
     - prolate symmetric tops
     - :math:`(J, K)` ladder, :math:`A > B = C`.
   * - ``asymmetricProlate``
     - H\ :sub:`2`\ O, O\ :sub:`3`, NO\ :sub:`2`, CH\ :sub:`2`
     - near-prolate :math:`(J, K)` approximation.

The governing energy expressions and degeneracies are given in
:doc:`../theory/rovibronic_levels`. Each species also carries its
distinct normal modes (harmonic frequency :math:`\omega_m`,
degeneracy :math:`d_m`, diagonal anharmonicity :math:`x_{mm}`) and,
where the literature value exists, the cross-mode anharmonicities
:math:`x_{ij}`. CO\ :sub:`2`, CH\ :sub:`4` and H\ :sub:`2`\ O carry
anharmonic sets (flagged NON-NIST, since CCCBDB tabulates no
anharmonic constants; the CH\ :sub:`4` :math:`x_{13}` is unavailable
and the CO\ :sub:`2` Fermi resonance is deperturbed), while the
remaining molecules have harmonic modes built on the CCCBDB
fundamentals. Every rotor branch applies the :math:`1/\sigma`
symmetry factor. The collision data of every polyatomic species are
Gosma's collision-specific rows, like those of the other neutrals.

Per-species model switches
``````````````````````````

The species data themselves cannot be changed from a case. The
optional ``moleculeProperties`` sub-dictionary of
``constant/dsmcProperties``, keyed by species name, accepts only two
model switches:

* ``allowSpinForbiddenTransitions`` (``true`` / ``false``) —  the
  electronic spin-conservation gate of the LB relaxation and of the
  equilibrium initialisation;
* ``vibrationalCoupling`` (``separable`` / ``coupled``) —  the
  polyatomic vibrational coupling, see
  :doc:`../theory/rovibronic_levels`.

Any of ``mass``, ``diameter``, ``omega``, ``alpha``, ``charge``,
``rotationalDegreesOfFreedom``, ``vibrationalModes``,
``maintainOddEvenParity``, ``internalEnergyList``,
``degeneracyList`` or ``dissociationEnergy`` stops the solver with
a ``FatalError``; so does a case without ``rovibronicDatabase``,
since the former path that took all species data from
``moleculeProperties`` has been removed.

Status of charged-species data
------------------------------

.. warning::

   The database entries for ions and the electron are present and
   parse cleanly, but the chemistry classes that consume them are
   part of the charged-species block that has not yet been
   verified and validated end-to-end (see :doc:`reactions`). The
   spectroscopic data itself can be trusted; its *use* in the
   reactive channels cannot yet be.

Extending the database
----------------------

A new species, or a change to an existing one, goes through the
source files and generators of ``speciesDatabase/sources/`` and
needs the code owner's approval; the procedure and the repository
lock are described in :doc:`species_database_sources`. Once the
species is in the database, a case selects it by appending its
symbol to ``typeIdList`` in ``constant/dsmcProperties``.
