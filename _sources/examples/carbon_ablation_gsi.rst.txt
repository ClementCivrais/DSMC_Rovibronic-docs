Carbon ablation on a heat shield (Mars)
=======================================

Directory: ``run/GSI/marsAblation/``.

A gas-surface-interaction case that exercises **finite-rate carbon
ablation** of a graphite heat shield in a Mars (CO\ :sub:`2`)
environment, using the ``dsmcAblatingWallPatch`` reactive wall and
the central ``surfaceChemReactDict``.

Theoretical basis
-----------------

* :doc:`../theory/wall_models` —  the finite-rate surface-reaction
  probability :math:`\beta(T_w) = \min(1, A\,T_w^{n}\,e^{-T_a/T_w})`,
  the carbon-oxidation and Boudouard channels, catalytic
  recombination, and the vapour-pressure sublimation of the
  carbon-vapour species.
* :doc:`../classes/boundaries` —  the reactive-wall classes
  (``dsmcCatalyticWallPatch``, ``dsmcAblatingWallPatch``,
  ``dsmcCoverageWallPatch``) and the surface-reaction dictionary
  format.

The reaction set comprises the Park oxidation
:math:`\mathrm{O} + \mathrm{C(s)} \to \mathrm{CO}`
(:math:`\beta = 0.63\,e^{-1160/T_w}`), the
:math:`\mathrm{O}_2 + \mathrm{C(s)} \to \mathrm{CO} + \mathrm{O}`
channel, the CO\ :sub:`2` Boudouard channel
:math:`\mathrm{CO}_2 + \mathrm{C(s)} \to 2\,\mathrm{CO}`
(Zhluktov-Abe), and catalytic O + O → O\ :sub:`2` recombination.

Validation goals
----------------

#. **Surface mass loss.** The carbon mass-loss rate
   :math:`\dot{m}_{\mathrm{C}}` must match the analytic surface
   law for the prescribed incident flux and wall temperature; the
   ``gsi_mars_ablation`` regression check pins
   :math:`\dot{m}_{\mathrm{C}} \approx 0.10~\mathrm{kg/m^2/s}`
   (20 % tolerance).
#. **Carbon-atom bookkeeping.** Each ablation event must debit the
   correct number of solid carbon atoms from the per-face ledger,
   and the emitted CO / CO products must conserve atoms.

Related GSI cases
-----------------

The GSI suite under ``run/GSI/`` also includes ``parkOxidation``
(oxidation efficiency sweep), ``catalysisGammaT`` (material
:math:`\gamma(T)` correlations for RCC / SiO\ :sub:`2` / SiC),
``catalysisKisliuk`` (precursor-mediated sticking),
``catalysisEnergy`` (recombination-energy accommodation),
``earthAblation`` and ``titanAblation`` (nitridation). The
immersed-level-set surface recession on an STL geometry is
demonstrated by ``run/3D/stlCapsule``.

Running the case
----------------

.. code-block:: bash

   cd run/GSI/marsAblation
   blockMesh
   dsmcInitialise
   dsmcFoam

Expected results
----------------

The ablating wall reports the per-face carbon consumed, the
recession rate and depth, and the CO production; the carbon
mass-loss rate matches the analytic surface law within the
regression tolerance. The ``D.gsi`` group of the
:doc:`../guide/regression_tests` gates the Park oxidation,
:math:`\gamma(T)` catalysis, Kisliuk sticking, Mars ablation, and
recombination-energy checks.
