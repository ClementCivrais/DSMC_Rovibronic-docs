Polyatomic thermal relaxation (CO\ :sub:`2`)
============================================

Directory: ``run/0D/CO2_relaxation/``.

A zero-dimensional adiabatic heat-bath case that exercises the
**multi-mode polyatomic relaxation** of a linear triatomic. Pure
CO\ :sub:`2` is initialised with a hot translational mode and a
cold internal state; the rotational and three vibrational modes
relax toward a single energy-conserving equilibrium temperature.

Theoretical basis
-----------------

The case rests on two theory pages:

* :doc:`../theory/rovibronic_levels` —  the polyatomic level
  structure: the linear rigid rotor (:math:`g = 2J+1`), the three
  vibrational modes of CO\ :sub:`2` (the symmetric stretch
  :math:`\nu_1`, the doubly-degenerate bend :math:`\nu_2`, the
  antisymmetric stretch :math:`\nu_3`), and the per-mode anharmonic
  ladders. Both the ``separable`` and the ``coupled`` (cross-mode
  :math:`x_{ij}`) treatments are available, and companion cases
  (``CO2sep_*``, ``CO2coupled_*``, ``CO2harm``) isolate each.
* :doc:`../theory/relaxation` —  the parallel-marginal cascade and
  the Larsen-Borgnakke kernel that drives the internal modes toward
  the bath temperature.

Validation goals
----------------

#. **Energy-conserving equilibrium.** The final state must satisfy
   :math:`T_{\mathrm{trans}} \approx T_{\mathrm{int}} \approx
   T_{\mathrm{eq}}`, with :math:`T_{\mathrm{eq}}` the temperature at
   which translational and full internal energy are partitioned at
   equilibrium. The regression golden value for this case is
   :math:`T_{\mathrm{eq}} = 1885~\mathrm{K}`.
#. **Detailed balance.** Started from
   :math:`T_{\mathrm{trans}} = T_{\mathrm{int}}` (the companion
   ``CO2_detbal`` case), the temperatures must not drift.
#. **Mode accounting.** The recovered effective vibrational DOF
   must reflect the full :math:`3N - 5 = 4` mode count, including
   the degeneracy of the bend.

Case parameters
---------------

* Species: CO\ :sub:`2` (``typeIdList (CO2);``).
* Number density: :math:`6.0\times10^{22}~\mathrm{m}^{-3}`.
* Translational temperature: :math:`6000~\mathrm{K}`.
* Internal temperature at :math:`t = 0`: :math:`300~\mathrm{K}`.
* Total integration: :math:`4\times10^{-6}~\mathrm{s}`.

Running the case
----------------

.. code-block:: bash

   cd run/0D/CO2_relaxation
   blockMesh
   dsmcInitialise
   dsmcFoam

Expected results
----------------

The translational temperature falls and the internal temperature
rises until both meet at :math:`T_{\mathrm{eq}} \approx
1885~\mathrm{K}`; total energy is conserved throughout. Switching
``vibrationalCoupling`` between ``separable`` and ``coupled`` in
``moleculeProperties`` shifts the equilibrium internal energy by
the cross-anharmonicity contribution, which the ``CO2coupled_*``
cases quantify. The relaxation of the eight polyatomic species
(CO\ :sub:`2`, CH\ :sub:`4`, H\ :sub:`2`\ O, CH\ :sub:`3`,
NH\ :sub:`3`, O\ :sub:`3`, N\ :sub:`2`\ O, HCN) is gated by the
``A.relax`` group of the :doc:`../guide/regression_tests`.
