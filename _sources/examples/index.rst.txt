Examples
========

The solver ships with a suite of reference tutorials under
``tutorials/`` in the source tree, together with focused
verification cases under ``run/0D/``, ``run/GSI/`` and ``run/3D/``.
Between them they cover the principal classes of DSMC simulation
targeted by the rovibronic extension: from zero-dimensional
heat-bath validation through micro-channel flows, external
hypersonic bodies, a representative re-entry capsule, and the
polyatomic, planetary-atmosphere and gas-surface-interaction
capabilities added in the current development cycle.

Every case shares the same three-fold OpenFOAM layout
(``0/`` field directory, ``constant/`` with ``dsmcProperties``
and ``speciesCache/``, ``system/`` with the run-time
dictionaries) and runs to completion through the standard
``blockMesh`` → ``dsmcInitialise`` → ``dsmcFoam`` pipeline.
