Saturn H\ :sub:`2`/He kinetics
==============================

Directories: ``run/0D/Saturn_H2_relax/`` (V-T relaxation) and
``run/0D/Saturn_H2_diss_rate/`` (dissociation rate).

Two zero-dimensional cases that exercise the **hydrogen-dominated
kinetics** of Saturn (and giant-planet) entry: the partner-
dependent vibration-translation relaxation of H\ :sub:`2`, and the
Higdon H\ :sub:`2` dissociation rate. They are the worked example
of the :doc:`../guide/planetary_atmospheres` page.

Theoretical basis
-----------------

* :doc:`../theory/relaxation` —  the temperature-dependent
  generalised Millikan-White :math:`Z_v(T)` with the Park high-T
  limiter, routed to the pure-vibrational (V-T) channel. The
  H\ :sub:`2` Millikan-White constants are mapped from Higdon
  Table 2 into ``vibB1`` / ``vibB2`` / ``vibTHT``.
* :doc:`../theory/chemistry` —  TCE dissociation, here driven
  directly by the Higdon Arrhenius rate.

Two features distinguish H\ :sub:`2` from air diatomics. First, its
large rotational quantum spacing means it does not obey Parker's
temperature-dependent law, so a **constant**
:math:`Z_{\mathrm{rot}} = 174` (Higdon) is used
(``rotationalRelaxationCollisionNumber 174``). Second, the
H\ :sub:`2`, H and He variable-hard-sphere parameters are supplied
as per-species overrides so the global database is untouched.

Validation goals
----------------

#. **Dissociation rate.** The measured rate of
   :math:`\mathrm{H}_2 + \mathrm{H}_2 \to 2\mathrm{H} +
   \mathrm{H}_2` must reproduce the Higdon Arrhenius form
   :math:`k(T) = 1.727\times10^{-11}\,T^{-1.0}\exp(-51958/T)`
   (m\ :sup:`3`/s); this is the ``chem_Saturn_H2`` regression
   check, with a 0.7–1.3 ratio window.
#. **V-T relaxation and detailed balance.** The vibrational mode
   must relax toward the bath on the Millikan-White / Higdon
   timescale, with no drift when started at equilibrium
   (``Saturn_H2_detbal``).

Case parameters
---------------

* Species: ``typeIdList (H2 He H);``.
* Relaxation case: translational temperature
  :math:`15\,000~\mathrm{K}`, internal temperature
  :math:`1000~\mathrm{K}`; integration
  :math:`1.5\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{s}`.
* Constant :math:`Z_{\mathrm{rot}} = 174`; H\ :sub:`2`
  ``vibrationalRelaxationModel valentini``.

Running the cases
-----------------

.. code-block:: bash

   cd run/0D/Saturn_H2_diss_rate     # or Saturn_H2_relax
   blockMesh
   dsmcInitialise
   dsmcFoam

Expected results
----------------

The dissociation case prints a reaction rate that matches the
Higdon Arrhenius value within the regression window; the relaxation
case shows the H\ :sub:`2` vibrational temperature approaching the
bath on the Higdon/Millikan-White timescale, with the constant
:math:`Z_{\mathrm{rot}} = 174` holding rotation slow relative to
air.
