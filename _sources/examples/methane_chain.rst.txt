Methane pyrolysis chain (Titan)
===============================

Directory: ``run/0D/CH4_dissoc_chain/``.

A zero-dimensional case that exercises the **polyatomic
dissociation cascade** of Titan entry, in which methane is stripped
of its hydrogens one at a time,
:math:`\mathrm{CH}_4 \to \mathrm{CH}_3 \to \mathrm{CH}_2 \to
\mathrm{CH} \to \mathrm{C}`, through a chain of
``polyDissociationTCE`` reactions (the final
:math:`\mathrm{CH} \to \mathrm{C} + \mathrm{H}` step uses the
diatomic ``dissociationTCE``).

Theoretical basis
-----------------

* :doc:`../theory/chemistry` —  polyatomic TCE dissociation, with
  the collision energy summed over all oscillators of the molecule,
  and the joint Bergemann-Boyd post-reaction redistribution of the
  product's rotation and every oscillator.
* :doc:`../theory/rovibronic_levels` —  the multi-mode vibrational
  ladders of CH\ :sub:`4` (a spherical top, :math:`g_{\mathrm{rot}}
  = (2J+1)^2`) and its CH\ :sub:`3` / CH\ :sub:`2` / CH fragments.

The forward steps use a collision-theory bond-energy rate
:math:`k(T) = A\,T^{1/2}\exp(-D_0/k_B T)` with bond energies from
the Active Thermochemical Tables, rather than the measured
low-pressure-limit fall-off rate, whose steep negative exponent
would stall the cascade at high temperature. The measured Baulch
methane rate is verified separately in ``run/0D/CH4_to_CH3_rate``,
and the CH\ :sub:`4` ↔ CH\ :sub:`3` + H detailed balance in
``run/0D/CH4_CH3_detbal``.

Validation goals
----------------

#. **Atom conservation.** The number of carbon and hydrogen nuclei
   must be conserved as the cascade proceeds (drift
   :math:`< 0.1\%`); this is the ``chem_CH4_chain`` regression
   check.
#. **Cascade progression.** The species populations must evolve in
   the expected order, CH\ :sub:`4` depleting first and atomic C
   accumulating last.

Case parameters
---------------

* Species: ``typeIdList (CH4 CH3 CH2 CH C H);``.
* Initial composition: pure CH\ :sub:`4` at
  :math:`6.0\times10^{22}~\mathrm{m}^{-3}`.
* Translational and internal temperature:
  :math:`25\,000~\mathrm{K}` (a hot, fully reactive bath).
* Total integration: :math:`3\times10^{-7}~\mathrm{s}`.

Running the case
----------------

.. code-block:: bash

   cd run/0D/CH4_dissoc_chain
   blockMesh
   dsmcInitialise
   dsmcFoam

Expected results
----------------

The methane population collapses, the intermediate radicals
CH\ :sub:`3`, CH\ :sub:`2` and CH rise and fall in sequence, and
atomic carbon and hydrogen accumulate, with the carbon and hydrogen
nuclei conserved to within statistical noise throughout.
