Adding a feature
================

We describe below the three most common extension paths. Any of
them should be accompanied by a short validation case under
``run/``, and ultimately by a regression case once the framework
described in :doc:`regression_tests` is in place.

Adding a new species
--------------------

The species database is frozen: masses, collision data and
spectroscopy are generated from stored sources and compiled into
``libDSMC_MNF``, and a case cannot supply or override them. Adding
or changing a species therefore needs the code owner's approval.
The procedure, detailed in
:doc:`../classes/species_database_sources`, is:

#. Store the source data in ``src/lagrangian/DSMC/speciesDatabase/sources/``:
   NIST WebBook, ASD or CCCBDB pages and ATcT values under
   ``nist/raw/`` (with their checksums), and the collision data
   next to the Gosma (2025) and Pfeiffer (2022) files.
#. Extend the assignment tables (``nist/tables/``), the
   species-to-formula map of ``build_transport_data.py``, and the
   structural fields in ``dsmcSpeciesDatabaseData.C``.
#. Run ``build_transport_data.py`` and
   ``build_spectroscopy_data.py``, then their ``--check`` modes.
#. Rebuild ``libDSMC_MNF`` and the solvers, and regenerate any
   ``constant/speciesCache`` that will be read.
#. Commit with ``DSMC_SPECIES_DB_UNLOCK=1``; the pre-commit hook
   refuses the commit otherwise.
#. Add the species symbol to ``typeIdList`` in a zero-dimensional
   heat-bath case under ``run/`` and confirm that the equilibrium
   populations match a Boltzmann distribution at the target
   translational temperature.

Adding a new reaction
---------------------

#. Identify the appropriate reaction class from
   :doc:`../classes/reactions`. For neutral chemistry
   (dissociation, recombination, exchange) the existing classes
   are well exercised; for charged-species chemistry the classes
   are present but flagged as work in progress, and adding a new
   reaction there also implies validating the surrounding
   infrastructure.
#. Heats of reaction and thresholds for dissociation,
   recombination, exchange and ionisation are derived
   automatically from the database and are not supplied by the
   user; a QK reaction entry names species only.
#. Register the reaction in ``system/chemReactDict`` with the
   appropriate ``reactionModel`` block.
#. Rebuild and run the associated validation case.
#. Cross-check detailed balance against the backward rate, which
   is derived through the equilibrium constant from the forward
   rate. Any systematic drift is usually symptomatic of either a
   missing partition-function entry or a wrong rovibronic
   cut-off.

Adding a new relaxation sub-model
---------------------------------

#. Implement the sub-model under
   ``src/lagrangian/DSMC/collisions/derived/``, following the
   existing Larsen-Borgnakke VHS/VSS examples
   (:doc:`../classes/collision_models`). The parallel-marginal
   cascade described in :doc:`../theory/relaxation` should be
   respected unless a reason to deviate is explicitly documented.
#. Wire the sub-model selector through the
   ``BinaryCollisionModel`` key in
   ``constant/dsmcProperties`` and expose any new coefficients in
   ``collisionProperties`` or in the model's own ``<Model>Coeffs``
   sub-dictionary. Collision data are read through the cloud's
   pair tables (``cloud.dPQ``, ``cloud.omegaPQ``,
   ``cloud.alphaPQ``), never recombined from species values.
#. Add a zero-dimensional relaxation validation case that checks
   the fitted collision number against the input at a few
   temperatures.
