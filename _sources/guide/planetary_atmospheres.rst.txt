Planetary atmospheres
=====================

The rovibronic extension is built for hypersonic entry into a range
of planetary atmospheres. Atmosphere support is realised in three
layers: the spectroscopic and collision **database** (species), the
zero-dimensional gas-phase **chemistry and relaxation** cases under
``run/0D/``, and the per-atmosphere reactive **walls** under
``run/GSI/``. The present page collects, atmosphere by atmosphere,
the species set and the principal chemistry, and documents the
H\ :sub:`2`/He kinetics that make Saturn entry a distinct case.

The Earth, Mars and Titan gas-phase chemistries are exercised by
the verification cases of :doc:`../examples/index` and the
regression suite (:doc:`regression_tests`).

Saturn H\ :sub:`2`/He kinetics
------------------------------

Hydrogen-dominated entry (Saturn, Jupiter, ice-giant probes)
requires three departures from air chemistry. They are configured
per-case in ``constant/dsmcProperties`` so that the global database
is unaffected (the ``run/0D/Saturn_H2_*`` cases).

**Dissociation rate (Higdon 2018).** The neutral H\ :sub:`2`
dissociation channels are taken from Higdon et al.
[Higdon2018]_ in the Arrhenius form
:math:`k(T) = A\,T^{\eta}\exp(-T_a/T)`, with the bond energy
:math:`D_0 = 4.478~\mathrm{eV}` (:math:`T_a = 51958~\mathrm{K}`):

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 40 30 15

   * - Reaction
     - :math:`A`  [m\ :sup:`3`/s]
     - :math:`\eta`
   * - H\ :sub:`2` + H\ :sub:`2` → 2H + H\ :sub:`2`
     - :math:`1.727\times10^{-11}`
     - :math:`-1.0`
   * - H\ :sub:`2` + H → 2H + H
     - :math:`1.386\times10^{-10}`
     - :math:`-1.0`
   * - H\ :sub:`2` + He → 2H + He
     - :math:`6.924\times10^{-12}`
     - :math:`-1.0`

**Constant rotational collision number.** Because H\ :sub:`2` is
extremely light, its rotational quantum spacing is large
(:math:`\theta_{\mathrm{rot}} \approx 87.6~\mathrm{K}`), and its
rotational relaxation does *not* follow Parker's temperature-
dependent law. Higdon fits a single constant
:math:`Z_{\mathrm{rot}} = 174`, which is set as
``rotationalRelaxationCollisionNumber 174`` in the Saturn cases.

**H\ :sub:`2` vibrational relaxation.** The Millikan-White
constants for the dominant H\ :sub:`2`-H\ :sub:`2` pair (Higdon
Table 2) are mapped to the generalised :math:`Z_v(T)` form of
:doc:`../theory/relaxation` (``vibrationalRelaxationModel
valentini``, with ``vibB1`` :math:`\approx 1.52\times10^{4}`,
``vibB2`` :math:`\approx 150`, ``vibTHT`` 50000), reproducing the
reference relaxation time to within :math:`\sim\!12\%` over
3000–20000 K.

**Pair-specific VHS.** The variable-hard-sphere diameters and
viscosity exponents for H\ :sub:`2`, H and He are taken from Higdon
Table 1 (:math:`T_{\mathrm{ref}} = 1000~\mathrm{K}`) and supplied
through per-species ``moleculeProperties`` overrides; unlike pairs
use the standard arithmetic-mean averaging rule.

.. [Higdon2018] K. J. Higdon, B. A. Cruden, A. M. Brandis,
   D. S. Liechty, D. B. Goldstein, and P. L. Varghese, "Direct
   simulation Monte Carlo shock simulation of Saturn entry probe
   conditions," *Journal of Thermophysics and Heat Transfer*,
   **32** (3), 680–690 (2018). Copy:
   ``docs/References/Higdon (2018) - Saturn.pdf``.
