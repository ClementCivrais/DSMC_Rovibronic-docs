dsmcFoam
========

``applications/solvers/discreteMethods/dsmcFoam``

Purpose
-------

``dsmcFoam`` is the transient, multi-species DSMC solver of the code. It
advances the simulator parcels on a fixed mesh in two, three or
axisymmetric dimensions. Every parcel carries its electronic, vibrational
and rotational quantum numbers :math:`(n, v, J)`, so the internal energy,
its relaxation and the chemistry are resolved state by state
(:doc:`../../theory/index`).

What the solver includes
------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 70

   * - Model family
     - Content
   * - Collisions
     - VHS and VSS cross sections with collision-specific pair data;
       no-time-counter pair selection
       (:doc:`../../theory/collision_cross_sections`,
       :doc:`../../theory/collision_sampling`)
   * - Internal energy
     - Larsen-Borgnakke relaxation of the rovibronic levels with the
       Nizenkov rate correction
       (:doc:`../../theory/larsen_borgnakke`, :doc:`../../theory/relaxation`)
   * - Chemistry
     - quantum-kinetic (QK) dissociation, SR-:math:`\mu`\ TST exchange,
       recombination by detailed balance, charged-species reactions
       (:doc:`../../theory/chemistry`)
   * - Walls
     - diffuse, specular, CLL, TD, impulsive and adiabatic kernels with an
       exact internal-state sampler; per-face wall temperature; catalytic,
       ablating and finite-rate coverage walls
       (:doc:`../../theory/wall_models`)
   * - Immersed surfaces
     - implicit (level set) and explicit (STL) surfaces on the fixed gas
       mesh, with probability or finite-rate chemistry and recession
       (:doc:`../../reference/gsi_verification/immersed_surface`)
   * - Measurements
     - cell, zone and boundary measurements, controllers, mesh adaptation

The receding body-fitted wall and the conjugate solid need a moving mesh
or a second region; they are in :doc:`dsmcFoamAblation`.

One time step
-------------

Each time step calls ``dsmcCloud::evolve()``:

#. update the time information of the boundaries, fields and
   controllers;
#. move the parcels for :math:`\Delta t`; wall impacts are handled by
   the wall models of ``system/boundariesDict`` during the move;
#. interactions with the immersed surface (reflection, chemistry,
   recession at its update interval);
#. cell occupancy and weighting, then the update of the internal-energy
   corrections;
#. collisions: pair selection, Larsen-Borgnakke relaxation and the
   reactions of ``system/chemReactDict``;
#. surface kinetics of the finite-rate walls, measurements, controllers
   and adaptation.

The figure below follows one gas-surface impact through these models.

.. figure:: ../../_static/figures/diagrams/impact_path.svg
   :width: 90%
   :alt: path of one gas-surface impact through the kernel, probability
         and finite-rate surface models, the energy and mass ledgers, the
         surface kinetics and the recession update

   One gas-surface impact. The surface model of the wall or of the
   immersed surface decides the outcome; every outcome is booked in the
   energy and mass ledgers. Between impacts the surface kinetics of each
   element advance every step; the removed bulk atoms drive the
   recession at each update.

Usage
-----

.. code-block:: bash

   blockMesh
   dsmcInitialise
   dsmcFoam > log.dsmcFoam 2>&1

   # in parallel
   decomposePar
   mpirun -np 4 dsmcFoam -parallel > log.dsmcFoam 2>&1
   reconstructPar

The random-number seed is taken from the clock unless ``randomSeed`` is
set in ``constant/dsmcProperties``; set it (and stagger the seeds of
replicas) for reproducible runs. On the same build and processor count a
seeded run is reproduced exactly.
