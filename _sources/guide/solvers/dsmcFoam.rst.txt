dsmcFoam
========

``applications/solvers/discreteMethods/dsmcFoam``

Purpose
-------

``dsmcFoam`` is the transient, multi-species DSMC solver of the code. It
advances the simulator parcels on a fixed mesh in two, three or
axisymmetric dimensions. Every parcel carries its electronic, vibrational
and rotational quantum numbers :math:`(n, v, J)`, so the internal energy,
its relaxation and the chemistry are resolved state by state
(:doc:`../../theory/index`).

What the solver includes
------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 70

   * - Model family
     - Content
   * - Collisions
     - VHS and VSS cross sections with collision-specific pair data;
       no-time-counter pair selection
       (:doc:`../../theory/collision_cross_sections`,
       :doc:`../../theory/collision_sampling`)
   * - Internal energy
     - Larsen-Borgnakke relaxation of the rovibronic levels with the
       Nizenkov rate correction
       (:doc:`../../theory/larsen_borgnakke`, :doc:`../../theory/relaxation`)
   * - Chemistry
     - quantum-kinetic (QK) dissociation, SR-:math:`\mu`\ TST exchange,
       recombination by detailed balance, charged-species reactions
       (:doc:`../../theory/chemistry`)
   * - Walls
     - diffuse, specular, CLL, TD, impulsive and adiabatic kernels with an
       exact internal-state sampler; per-face wall temperature; catalytic,
       ablating and finite-rate coverage walls
       (:doc:`../../theory/wall_models`)
   * - Immersed surfaces
     - implicit (level set) and explicit (STL) surfaces on the fixed gas
       mesh, with probability or finite-rate chemistry and recession
       (:doc:`../../reference/gsi_verification/immersed_surface`)
   * - Measurements
     - cell, zone and boundary measurements, controllers, mesh adaptation

The receding body-fitted wall and the conjugate solid need a moving mesh
or a second region; they are in :doc:`dsmcFoam`.

One time step
-------------

Each time step calls ``dsmcCloud::evolve()``:

#. update the time information of the boundaries, fields and
   controllers;
#. move the parcels for :math:`\Delta t`; wall impacts are handled by
   the wall models of ``system/boundariesDict`` during the move;
#. interactions with the immersed surface (reflection, chemistry,
   recession at its update interval);
#. cell occupancy and weighting, then the update of the internal-energy
   corrections;
#. collisions: pair selection, Larsen-Borgnakke relaxation and the
   reactions of ``system/chemReactDict``;
#. surface kinetics of the finite-rate walls, measurements, controllers
   and adaptation.

The figure below follows one gas-surface impact through these models.

.. figure:: ../../_static/figures/diagrams/impact_path.svg
   :width: 90%
   :alt: path of one gas-surface impact through the kernel, probability
         and finite-rate surface models, the energy and mass ledgers, the
         surface kinetics and the recession update

   One gas-surface impact. The surface model of the wall or of the
   immersed surface decides the outcome; every outcome is booked in the
   energy and mass ledgers. Between impacts the surface kinetics of each
   element advance every step; the removed bulk atoms drive the
   recession at each update.

Usage
-----

.. code-block:: bash

   blockMesh
   dsmcInitialise
   dsmcFoam > log.dsmcFoam 2>&1

   # in parallel
   decomposePar
   mpirun -np 4 dsmcFoam -parallel > log.dsmcFoam 2>&1
   reconstructPar

The random-number seed is taken from the clock unless ``randomSeed`` is
set in ``constant/dsmcProperties``; set it (and stagger the seeds of
replicas) for reproducible runs. On the same build and processor count a
seeded run is reproduced exactly.

Receding walls and conjugate solid
----------------------------------
The same executable simulates a gas flow over a body whose surface
reacts, heats, melts and recedes:

- **receding body-fitted walls**: an ablating wall
  (``dsmcAblatingWallPatch``) or a finite-rate coverage wall with bulk
  reactions (``dsmcCoverageWallPatch``) moves the gas mesh as it loses
  material;
- **conjugate solid**: heat conduction in the solid with
  temperature-dependent properties, re-radiation, melting, a melt film
  and droplets, and recession of the solid and gas meshes together; the
  solid sets the wall temperature seen by the gas.

Without ``constant/dynamicMeshDict`` (or with a ``staticFvMesh``) and
without ``constant/conjugateProperties`` the solver advances the gas
only. The immersed receding surfaces are a feature of the particle cloud
and need no moving mesh.

The coupling stays inside OpenFOAM: the solid regions are meshes of the
same case on the same ``Time``, decomposed with the gas
(``decomposePar -allRegions``); no external library is linked.

Time loop
~~~~~~~~~

Each time step of ``dsmcFoam.C``:

#. a receding body-fitted wall converts this step's recession depth into
   a displacement of its points along the current point normals;
#. the gas mesh is moved (``mesh.update()``, skipped inside a coupling
   window when the conjugate solid moves the wall); each parcel keeps its
   position in its tetrahedron, so no parcel is left in the solid;
#. ``dsmc.evolve()`` advances the gas exactly as in :doc:`dsmcFoam`, with
   the walls adding their heat, mass and momentum to the window sums;
#. at the end of a coupling window the solid regions are advanced and
   the wall temperature and displacement are returned to the gas
   (``conjugateRegionsCoupling.H``).

.. figure:: ../../_static/figures/diagrams/coupling_window.svg
   :width: 100%
   :alt: gas and solid time lines, with N DSMC steps per window, the
         window-averaged exchange to the solid, M solid steps, and the
         wall temperature and displacement returned to the gas

   The coupling window. During :math:`N` DSMC steps the coupled walls
   accumulate the heat flux :math:`q`, the mass flux :math:`\dot m` and the
   force :math:`F` per face. At the end of the window these are mapped to
   the solid, which takes :math:`M` steps of :math:`\Delta t_s`; the wall
   temperature :math:`T_w` and the wall displacement come back to the gas
   before the next window.

Solid model
~~~~~~~~~~~

Each solid region solves the transient heat equation in enthalpy form on
its own moving mesh,

.. math::

   \rho\,\frac{\partial h(T)}{\partial t} = \nabla\cdot\big(k(T)\,\nabla T\big),
   \qquad h(T) = \int_0^T c_p(T')\,\mathrm{d}T',

with :math:`k(T)` and :math:`c_p(T)` any OpenFOAM ``Function1`` of the
temperature. The time term is written over the solid step
:math:`\Delta t_s` as
:math:`\rho\,[h(T)V - h(T^0)V^0]/(\Delta t_s V)`, linearised about the
current iterate in the outer correctors, and the mesh flux of the moving
solid mesh enters as an arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian term.

On a coupled face that is not melting, the conducted flux balances the
gas heat flux and the re-radiation (a Robin condition, linearised about
the face temperature),

.. math::

   k\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial n}\Big|_w = q - \varepsilon\sigma\left(T_w^4 - T_\mathrm{bg}^4\right).

A face that reaches :math:`T_\mathrm{melt}` is held there and melts at the
speed given by the Stefan condition,

.. math::

   \rho L\,v_\mathrm{melt} = q - \varepsilon\sigma\left(T_\mathrm{melt}^4 - T_\mathrm{bg}^4\right)
   - k\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial n}\Big|_\mathrm{solid},

and the outer correctors repeat until no face changes state. Without a
melt film the melt leaves at once and the wall recedes at
:math:`v_\mathrm{melt} + \dot m/\rho`, where :math:`\dot m` is the
chemical mass flux of the wall. With ``meltFilm`` the liquid stays on
the wall as a thin film driven by the gas shear and pressure,
capillarity and a body force, freezes again where the solid takes the
heat, and leaves as droplets that the gas carries away.

Gas-solid exchange
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Every coupled wall accumulates, per face and at :math:`O(1)` cost per
impact, the window energy (incident minus re-emitted energy in the wall
frame, absorbed and injected parcels, and the chemical energy where it is
deposited), the momentum and the removed mass. At the end of the window
these become the heat flux :math:`q`, the force density and the mass flux
:math:`\dot m` of each gas face. A face-area-weighted AMI maps them to the
solid faces conservatively,

.. math::

   q_s A_s = \sum_g q_g A_g\,\frac{A_{gs}}{\sum_{s'} A_{gs'}},

so the heat load of the patch is kept to round-off; the temperatures
return to the gas faces as area averages. The meshes may be
non-conformal. The solid wall points move by the least-squares offset of
their faces; each gas wall point is located on the solid surface and
moves with it.

Case layout
~~~~~~~~~~~

.. code-block:: text

   case/
     constant/
       dynamicMeshDict          gas mesh motion (ablationRecession for a
                                receding wall without the solid)
       conjugateProperties      solid regions, couplings, window, film
       regionProperties         regions ( gas (region0) solid (solid) );
       solid/polyMesh, solid/dynamicMeshDict, solid/solidProperties
     system/
       solid/fvSchemes, solid/fvSolution, solid/decomposeParDict
     0/
       solid/T                  mixed on the coupled patches
       solid/pointDisplacement  fixedValue on the coupled patches

The keywords of ``conjugateProperties``, ``solidProperties`` and
``ablationRecession`` are listed in :doc:`../input_reference` (section
"Gas-surface interaction dictionaries"). The couplings must be disjoint:
no patch in two couplings and no point shared by two coupled patches of
one mesh.

Usage
~~~~~

.. code-block:: bash

   blockMesh
   blockMesh -region solid
   dsmcInitialise
   dsmcFoam > log.dsmcFoam 2>&1

   # in parallel
   decomposePar -allRegions
   mpirun -np 4 dsmcFoam -parallel > log.dsmcFoam 2>&1
   reconstructPar -allRegions

Outputs
~~~~~~~

- ``postProcessing/conjugate/<startTime>/<coupling>.dat``: one line per
  window, with the window heat load and its relative difference between
  the gas and solid sides of the AMI;
- ``postProcessing/conjugate/<coupling>/<time>/wallData``: the face data
  at the write times;
- ``<time>/solid/``: the solid temperature, the melting state and the
  moved solid mesh;
- the gas fields and wall measurements of :doc:`dsmcFoam`.

Restart
~~~~~~~

``<time>/solid/uniform/solidTime`` holds the solid clock, the window
counters and the ledger; the fields ``vMelt`` and ``melting`` hold the
state of the coupled faces, and the ``pointDisplacement`` conditions the
accumulated displacements. The window sums of the walls are restarted
with them. The gas sample of an unfinished window is not restarted.

Limitations
~~~~~~~~~~~

- No pyrolysis, porosity or in-depth decomposition (charring ablators).
- No anisotropic conductivity, no mushy zone inside the solid, no
  radiative exchange between faces.
- Adjacent couplings (patches sharing points) are refused.
- The body-fitted mesh motion has no remeshing; strongly graded meshes
  fold after large recession. Large recession is the domain of the
  immersed surfaces, which have no solid under them yet (planned).
- The window accelerates the solid clock; this assumes a quasi-steady
  gas over a window.
