dsmcInitialise
==============

``applications/utilities/preProcessing/DSMC/dsmcInitialise``

Purpose
-------

``dsmcInitialise`` fills the gas mesh with simulator parcels before the
first run. Each zone of ``system/dsmcInitialiseDict`` sets the number
density of every species, the translational and internal temperatures
and the bulk velocity; the parcels are drawn from the corresponding
equilibrium distributions, with their electronic, vibrational and
rotational quantum numbers :math:`(n, v, J)` sampled from the rovibronic
level tables of the species database (:doc:`../../theory/rovibronic_levels`).

When ``databasePath`` is set in ``constant/dsmcProperties``, the utility
also writes the level tables of the atomic and diatomic species to
``constant/speciesCache/``. The solvers recompute the tables at
start-up; the caches are read by the Python oracles of the verification
cases, so they must be regenerated after any change of the species
database (see :ref:`the species cache entry <input-species-cache>` of the
input reference).

Usage
-----

.. code-block:: bash

   blockMesh
   dsmcInitialise
   # then dsmcFoam or dsmcFoamAblation

For a parallel run, decompose after the initialisation
(``decomposePar``, or ``decomposePar -allRegions`` for a case with solid
regions).

Inputs
------

- ``system/dsmcInitialiseDict``: the initialisation zones
  (:doc:`../input_reference`);
- ``constant/dsmcProperties``: the species, the simulator weight
  ``nEquivalentParticles`` and the model choices;
- the mesh in ``constant/polyMesh``.

Outputs
-------

- the parcels in ``0/lagrangian/dsmc``;
- the species caches in ``constant/speciesCache`` (when requested).
