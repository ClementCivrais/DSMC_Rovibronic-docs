Solvers and coupling
====================

The code provides one pre-processing utility and one solver, both built
on the same DSMC library (``src/lagrangian/DSMC``): the particle cloud,
the rovibronic species database, the collision, relaxation and chemistry
models, the wall models and the immersed surfaces. The solver
``dsmcFoam`` also advances receding body-fitted walls on a moving mesh
and the conjugate solid (heat conduction, melting, melt film and
droplets) when the case asks for them.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 22 18 60

   * - Executable
     - Type
     - Role
   * - :doc:`dsmcInitialise`
     - utility
     - fills the gas domain with parcels; writes the species caches
   * - :doc:`dsmcFoam`
     - solver
     - transient DSMC: rovibronic relaxation, QK chemistry, every wall
       model, immersed receding surfaces, receding body-fitted walls on a
       moving mesh, and solid regions coupled in the same executable
       (heat conduction, melting, melt film and droplets)

   Structure of ``dsmcFoam``. Arrows inside the frame are the
   data exchanged during a run: impacts and products between the gas and
   the surfaces, the window-averaged heat flux :math:`q`, mass flux
   :math:`\dot m` and force :math:`F` from the walls to the solid, the
   wall temperature :math:`T_w` and the wall displacement back to the gas.
   The planned blocks are not available yet.
