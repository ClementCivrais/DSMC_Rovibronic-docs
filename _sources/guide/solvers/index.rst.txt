Solvers and coupling
====================

The code provides one pre-processing utility and two solvers. All three
are built on the same DSMC library (``src/lagrangian/DSMC``): the
particle cloud, the rovibronic species database, the collision,
relaxation and chemistry models, the wall models and the immersed
surfaces are shared, so a case set up for ``dsmcFoam`` runs unchanged
in ``dsmcFoamAblation``. The second solver adds what needs a moving
mesh or a second region: receding body-fitted walls and the conjugate
solid (heat conduction, melting, melt film and droplets).

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 22 18 60

   * - Executable
     - Type
     - Role
   * - :doc:`dsmcInitialise`
     - utility
     - fills the gas domain with parcels; writes the species caches
   * - :doc:`dsmcFoam`
     - solver
     - transient DSMC on a fixed mesh: rovibronic relaxation, QK
       chemistry, every wall model, immersed receding surfaces
   * - :doc:`dsmcFoamAblation`
     - solver
     - ``dsmcFoam`` on a dynamic mesh, coupled in the same executable to
       solid regions: wall recession, heat conduction, melting, melt film
       and droplets

The figure below shows how the parts of ``dsmcFoamAblation`` exchange
data. The gas region and the gas-surface interaction models are those of
``dsmcFoam``; the solid regions and the gas mesh motion are the additions
of ``dsmcFoamAblation``. The external codes on the right are used for
verification only: no external library is linked.

.. figure:: ../../_static/figures/diagrams/framework_overview.svg
   :width: 100%
   :alt: dsmcFoamAblation, its gas region, gas-surface interaction models,
         solid regions and gas mesh motion, with the input databases,
         the outputs, the external verification codes and the planned
         blocks

   Structure of ``dsmcFoamAblation``. Arrows inside the frame are the
   data exchanged during a run: impacts and products between the gas and
   the surfaces, the window-averaged heat flux :math:`q`, mass flux
   :math:`\dot m` and force :math:`F` from the walls to the solid, the
   wall temperature :math:`T_w` and the wall displacement back to the gas.
   The planned blocks are not available yet.
